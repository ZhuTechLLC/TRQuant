# 拓普集团 601689.SH — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / CHALLENGER`.

## Decision question
Can Tuopu use its automotive scale/cash engine to build economically material humanoid actuator and AI/data-center liquid-cooling businesses fast enough to reaccelerate earnings, while avoiding the analytical mistake of combining separate “robot” and “liquid cooling” initiatives into one order thesis?

## What is established
- FY2025 revenue ~RMB29.58bn (+11.21%), attributable NP ~RMB2.78bn (-7.38%), CFO ~RMB4.48bn (+38.5%).
- 2026Q1 revenue ~RMB6.63bn (+14.92%), NP ~RMB552m (-2.42%), CFO ~RMB547m (-38.36%).
- 2025H1 company materials described new liquid-cooling/server products and a first batch of orders totaling ~RMB1.5bn across the new-field initiative; disclosures do **not** establish that RMB1.5bn as “robot liquid-cooling orders”.
- Humanoid actuator business is real but remained small relative to the automotive group base in 2025.

## Thesis
Tuopu is a useful **cross-mechanism challenger**: a profitable, cash-generative manufacturer with both robotics and liquid-cooling optionality, at a materially lower valuation than many pure-theme names. The thesis requires separate conversion bridges for robot actuators and AI/server thermal products.

## Counter-evidence
- 2025 NP declined and Q1 2026 NP remained slightly negative YoY.
- Theme revenue remains small relative to a RMB30bn group.
- The RMB1.5bn new-field order figure must not be labeled robot liquid-cooling orders.
- Automotive customer/cycle concentration remains relevant.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 reference ~RMB46.92/share, ~RMB81.5bn market cap, ~30x trailing P/E. At 25x 2028 P/E, ~RMB4.31bn 2028 NP would be needed for ~15% annualized two-year return versus FY2025 NP ~RMB2.78bn.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** automotive base stabilizes, robot actuator volumes become material and AI liquid-cooling orders scale with attractive incremental margins.
- **Base:** new fields grow but remain too small to offset muted legacy earnings; stock compounds near earnings growth.
- **Bear:** new fields are slow/low-margin while auto earnings soften and capex rises.

## Kill criteria
1. Robot/AI-liquid-cooling programs fail to become separately material in revenue/profit.
2. Automotive base cash generation deteriorates structurally.
3. New-field capex rises without incremental return evidence.
4. Market assigns theme multiple without earnings contribution.
