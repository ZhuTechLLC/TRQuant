# 飞荣达（300602.SZ）Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Market:** CN_A / ChiNext  
**Status:** `research_complete` — not `decision_grade`  
**Current action:** **WATCH / no default new capital**  
**Next major review:** 2026-08-26 NVIDIA + U.S. macro evidence node; 2026-08-28 liquid-cooling joint review

## 1. Executive conclusion

飞荣达 is best understood as a **cross-domain thermal-management platform with AI liquid cooling as the current earnings-growth engine and humanoid-robot liquid cooling as a high-upside option**. The case is stronger than a generic “AI liquid-cooling concept stock” because the company already has broad thermal-management revenue, batch-delivered server/data-center products, a widening liquid-cooling stack, and 2026H1 earnings acceleration. The case is also stronger than a pure robot-theme story because robot revenue remains small and is not required to support the current operating thesis.

The stock, however, is **not cheap enough to ignore execution quality**. At the 2026-08-21 close of RMB 41.57, market capitalization was about RMB 24.36bn. 2026H1 attributable net profit was RMB 252.6m; simple H1 annualization implies roughly 48x earnings, while the observed TTM P/E was about 54x. The market is already paying for sustained AI-liquid-cooling growth and some future-option value.

The load-bearing issue is therefore not “is liquid cooling a real trend?” It is:

> **Can 飞荣达 convert rising AI liquid-cooling demand into revenue, gross-margin improvement and cash generation fast enough to justify a high multiple, while robot liquid cooling remains upside rather than a valuation dependency?**

Current answer: **business thesis positive, stock attractiveness conditional, action WATCH.** A small starter becomes reviewable after either (a) a meaningful price reset with fundamentals intact or (b) evidence that AI liquid-cooling revenue/margins and cash conversion are stronger than currently observable.

## 2. Business architecture: four thermal-management demand pools

### A. AI servers and data centers — current P0 growth engine

2026H1 company disclosure says AI-server cooling demand grew rapidly, cooperation with multiple important customers advanced, orders continued to rise, and related revenue grew both year-over-year and sequentially. The server/data-center portfolio includes TIM, conventional heat sinks and fans, 3D VC, single-phase and two-phase liquid cold-plate modules, flow-control instruments, CDU, quick connectors, liquid-cooling chassis and liquid-cooling racks. Multiple categories were already in batch delivery, while additional high-value projects remained in sampling/pilot production.

This is economically important because liquid cooling adds new BOM layers rather than merely replacing a fan: cold plates / microchannels, manifolds, pumps, quick-disconnects, sensors, CDU and facility-side heat rejection. Increasing rack power density therefore creates both a penetration-rate tailwind and a content-per-rack tailwind.

### B. Consumer electronics — mature cash-generating technology base

飞荣达’s legacy know-how in TIM, graphite, VC/3D VC, heat pipes, fans, phase-change materials and increasingly active micro-pump cooling gives the company a technology and manufacturing base that can migrate into AI devices and robots. 2026H1 management attributed consumer-electronics growth to higher content per high-end device and share gains despite weak aggregate smartphone/PC volumes.

### C. New energy / storage / automotive — diversification and thermal-management adjacency

The portfolio includes battery liquid-cooling plates, DCDC wind/liquid heat exchangers, PCS cooling, supercharging thermal products and automotive electronic liquid cooling. 2026H1 management said already-awarded auto projects continued to release revenue and storage liquid-cooling orders were being delivered.

### D. Humanoid and industrial robots — future growth option, not current earnings pillar

The 2026H1 report explicitly added a **robot joint liquid-cooling module**: microchannel/embedded flow path + micro-pump + rotary sealing joint + external radiator, designed for high-power-density motors and reducers. The company also offers TIM, fans, lightweight structures, joint modules, dexterous-hand and robot-related products. But management explicitly states embodied-intelligence revenue is still a small share and does not materially affect current results.

This distinction is essential: **robot cooling validates technology optionality; AI/data-center cooling must carry the near-term earnings case.**

### Product economics matter more than headline margin

The broad **thermal-management materials and devices** category generated RMB 1.383bn in H1, +27.52% YoY and about **37.3% of group revenue**, but gross margin was only **15.08%, down 1.62pp**. This is the strongest counter-evidence to a simplistic claim that “liquid cooling automatically raises margin.” The thermal segment includes multiple products and the company does not disclose AI-liquid-cooling standalone revenue or standalone margin, so we cannot infer that liquid cooling itself is low margin; however, the consolidated thermal category does not yet show premium economics.

The company’s electromagnetic-shielding business had a much higher 31.43% gross margin in H1. Therefore total-company margin improvement cannot be attributed mechanically to AI liquid cooling.

### Cash conversion is the principal financial-quality watch item

H1 CFO / attributable net profit was about **73%**, versus CFO exceeding net profit for full-year 2025. Management attributes H1 CFO decline to strategic stocking. Inventory rose to RMB 1.425bn from RMB 1.114bn at 2025 year-end (+28.0%), broadly consistent with revenue growth but still cash-consuming. Accounts receivable reached RMB 3.021bn, about 30.3% of assets and roughly 81% of H1 revenue; this is not automatically a credit problem but makes collection and working-capital normalization load-bearing evidence.

A rough cash-flow proxy of CFO minus cash paid for PP&E/intangibles is negative in H1 (~RMB -77m), so current accounting-profit acceleration has not yet translated into strong free-cash-flow conversion during the expansion phase.

This is **not a net-cash quality compounder**. It is a manufacturing platform investing through an up-cycle. Liquidity is manageable, but earnings growth must eventually improve cash generation; otherwise a high P/E plus rising working capital/debt would become a poor capital-conversion setup.

The company also continues using a 2023 RMB 1bn equity-financing program; by 2026H1 the related committed projects had used ~RMB 611.9m cumulatively, with RMB 335m temporarily used to supplement working capital. This reinforces the need to judge growth by incremental ROIC and cash conversion, not revenue alone.

## 5. Why AI liquid cooling is structurally attractive

### Rack power density is forcing a technology transition

At NVIDIA GTC 2026, the Supermicro presentation on Vera Rubin described rack power around 227kW and explicitly characterized liquid cooling as moving “from optional to mandatory”; Vera Rubin was described as requiring 100% liquid cooling. The same presentation discussed a potential path toward 400–500kW racks and eventually still higher density.

This is a stronger demand signal than generic AI sentiment. The causal chain is:

`AI model demand -> hyperscaler/sovereign compute capex -> accelerator/rack deployment -> rack power density -> liquid-cooling attach rate -> thermal-management BOM -> supplier revenue -> margin/FCF`

The highest-value investment question is whether 飞荣达 captures only commodity cold-plate manufacturing or increasingly captures **system content**: cold plates + flow monitoring + CDU + connectors + chassis/rack + pump integration.

### Global benchmark shows what high-quality conversion can look like

Vertiv Q2 2026: sales +24%, adjusted operating profit +51%, adjusted operating margin 22.6%, adjusted EPS +60%, FCF +234%; full-year organic-sales-growth guidance ~30–32%.

nVent Q2 2026: sales +53%, organic +47%, adjusted EPS +69%, FCF +125%, full-year organic-growth guidance 32–34%; it announced a third liquid-cooling manufacturing expansion in three years.

These are not direct valuation comparables, but they establish a quality benchmark: **rapid sales growth should eventually produce operating leverage and cash flow, not only order headlines.** 飞荣达’s H1 operating momentum is promising, but its thermal-category gross margin and cash conversion remain below that benchmark quality.

## 6. Robot liquid cooling: strategic option and 华科冷芯 bridge

As of June 2026, 飞荣达 disclosed a **7.7783% stake in 华科冷芯（上海）动力科技有限公司** and stated that the two companies are integrating suspended-pump technology to provide liquid-cooling systems or thermal modules.

This matters for two reasons:

1. It gives 飞荣达 access to a specialized pump technology node rather than limiting it to cold plates/materials.
2. 华科冷芯 pump technology received highly visible real-world validation in the 2026 Beijing Yizhuang humanoid-robot marathon ecosystem.

But the equity stake is small and robot revenue is currently immaterial. Therefore **do not capitalize 华科冷芯 as if it were a controlled subsidiary, and do not use robot liquid cooling to justify the whole current market cap.** Treat it as an option whose value increases only after design-ins, repeat orders, revenue and margin evidence appear.

The stock had also moved from RMB 31.72 on 2026-07-31 to RMB 41.57 on 2026-08-21, a rise of about 31%, so some AI/robot-liquid-cooling attention has already diffused into price.

### Simple 2026 earnings sensitivity — not company guidance

H1 attributable NP = RMB 252.6m.

- H2 = H1 -> FY26 ~RMB 505m -> ~48x P/E at current market cap.
- H2 ~20% above H1 -> FY26 ~RMB 556m -> ~44x.
- These are analytical sensitivities, not forecasts.

### Reverse valuation: what earnings are required?

At current ~RMB 24.36bn market cap:

- To support the same market cap at **30x** P/E in 2028 requires ~RMB **812m** net profit.
- To earn roughly **15% annualized for two years** and exit at 30x requires ~RMB **1.074bn** 2028 net profit.
- At a 35x exit multiple, the same 15% return requires ~RMB **921m**.
- At a 25x exit multiple, it requires ~RMB **1.289bn**.

Relative to simple H1 annualized earnings (~RMB 505m), the 15%-return requirement implies roughly **35%–60% two-year earnings CAGR**, depending on exit multiple. That is achievable only if AI cooling remains high-growth and operating leverage/cash conversion improve; the current price leaves limited room for execution disappointment.

## 8. Scenario framework — analytical, not forecast

### Bear

Conditions:
- U.S./China AI infrastructure capex or server deployments slow materially.
- Liquid-cooling mix rises but pricing/competition prevents margin improvement.
- Thermal-management gross margin remains ~15% or falls.
- Receivables/inventory continue consuming cash.
- Robot products remain pre-scale.

2028 analytical NP range: RMB 0.65–0.75bn; exit P/E 25–28x.  
Implied equity value: ~RMB 16–21bn, below current market cap.

### Base

Conditions:
- AI server/data-center liquid cooling remains a multi-year growth cycle.
- 飞荣达 expands from components into higher-content liquid-cooling systems.
- Thermal-category margin improves gradually and working capital normalizes.
- Consumer/new-energy businesses remain supportive.
- Robot cooling remains small but starts contributing evidence of design-in/repeat orders.

2028 analytical NP range: RMB 0.95–1.10bn; exit P/E 30–35x.  
Implied equity value: ~RMB 28.5–38.5bn.

### Bull

Conditions:
- AI rack density and domestic/overseas liquid-cooling penetration accelerate faster than base case.
- Higher-value CDU/flow-control/connectors/pump-integrated modules become meaningful revenue.
- Margin and FCF scale toward global infrastructure benchmark direction.
- Robot joint cooling moves from option to second growth curve with repeat production orders.

2028 analytical NP range: RMB 1.25–1.45bn; exit P/E 35–40x.  
Implied equity value: ~RMB 44–58bn.

These ranges are deliberately broad because the company does not disclose standalone AI-liquid-cooling revenue or margin and there is no issuer 2028 earnings guidance.

## 9. Macro and market transmission

### U.S. macro is both a demand support and a valuation risk

The July 2026 FOMC held the federal-funds target at 3.50–3.75%; three voters preferred a 25bp hike. July CPI was 3.4% YoY and core CPI 2.5%; Q2 real GDP advance estimate was only 1.5% annualized versus 2.1% in Q1. This creates a difficult combination: AI capex can remain strong while the discount rate stays high.

For 飞荣达, U.S. macro transmits through two channels:

1. **Operating demand:** U.S. hyperscaler and GPU-system capex supports global AI-server liquid cooling, including Chinese supply-chain read-through.
2. **Valuation/risk appetite:** high U.S. long rates and AI-ROI concerns compress multiples of high-duration AI infrastructure stocks globally, including A-share thematic peers.

A strong liquid-cooling industry therefore does not guarantee a rising stock price. `EPS revision - multiple compression` must be evaluated separately.

### China overlay

Domestic AI infrastructure, storage, EV and embodied-intelligence policy support are favorable structural channels. But broad domestic demand is less important than the company-specific ability to convert design-ins into batch revenue, margin and cash. Policy or theme strength should never substitute for revenue-quality evidence.

## 10. Competitive positioning

### Strengths

- Broad thermal stack from materials to liquid-cooling modules/system components.
- Actual batch delivery in server/data-center categories.
- Cross-domain manufacturing base: consumer, telecom, server, auto/storage, robotics.
- Strong customer network and long history with major electronics/telecom OEMs.
- R&D intensity around 5–6% of revenue and rising absolute R&D.
- Robot liquid-cooling product is explicitly in the product portfolio, not merely a conceptual application.
- 华科冷芯 relationship adds pump-system integration capability.

### Weaknesses / unresolved questions

- No standalone AI-liquid-cooling revenue, gross margin, order backlog or customer concentration disclosure.
- Broad thermal-management gross margin is only 15.08% in H1 and declined YoY.
- H1 CFO fell despite strong profit growth; inventory and receivables are high.
- Manufacturing business remains capital intensive and leveraged relative to net-cash peers.
- Robot revenue remains immaterial; valuation cannot rely on it today.
- High current multiple means operating-quality evidence must keep improving.

## 11. Decision state

### Business thesis

**POSITIVE / validated at industry and company-product level.** AI liquid cooling is a real current growth vector; robot liquid cooling is a credible option.

### Stock attractiveness

**MODERATE / price-sensitive.** The company has stronger current earnings evidence than many robot-liquid-cooling concepts, but ~54x TTM P/E and recent price acceleration already price in substantial success.

### Current action

**WATCH — default new capital = 0 under current TRQuant capital rules.**

A **START SMALL review** becomes appropriate under either path:

1. **Price path:** approximately RMB 35 or lower while AI cooling demand, customer delivery and earnings evidence remain intact; or
2. **Evidence path:** at higher prices, new disclosure shows materially stronger AI-liquid-cooling revenue/margin/cash conversion such that 2028 base earnings are credibly pushed above ~RMB 1.0–1.1bn.

Do not chase a robot-liquid-cooling headline alone.

### Add / upgrade conditions

- Standalone or inferable AI-liquid-cooling revenue grows materially faster than company revenue.
- Thermal-management gross margin reverses upward while high-value liquid-cooling mix rises.
- CFO/NP returns above ~1x on a rolling basis or working-capital build is clearly converted into sales/cash.
- CDU/flow-control/quick-connect/pump-integrated content becomes repeat batch revenue.
- Major AI infrastructure customers maintain/raise real compute deployment plans.
- Robot cooling achieves named or otherwise auditable design-ins and repeat production orders without margin dilution.

### Kill / downgrade criteria

- Two or more quarters of material AI-server/liquid-cooling order deterioration without a credible cyclical explanation.
- Thermal-management revenue grows but gross margin continues structurally downward, indicating commodity price competition.
- Persistent CFO weakness, rising receivables/inventory and debt despite reported profit growth.
- Material loss of key server/telecom customers or failure to convert sample/pilot projects into batch orders.
- Robot narrative becomes a major valuation pillar while robot revenue/order evidence remains immaterial.
- U.S. AI capex undergoes a broad real-compute cut at multiple hyperscalers, accompanied by weakening GPU/rack deployments.
