## Why this is not a generic robot-theme case

The current earnings engine is AI/server/data-center thermal management, not humanoid robots. 2026H1 revenue grew 28.64%, attributable net profit 52.09%, and deducted net profit 91.30%. The company disclosed rising server/data-center cooling orders and batch delivery across multiple product categories. By contrast, management explicitly states embodied-intelligence revenue remains a small share and does not materially affect current results.

Robot joint liquid cooling is therefore recorded as a **future option**. It becomes load-bearing only after auditable design-ins, repeat production orders, revenue and margin appear.

## Dominant variable

The dominant variable is:

`AI liquid-cooling demand -> Feirongda design-ins/system content -> batch revenue -> thermal gross margin -> working-capital conversion -> FCF -> earnings revisions -> valuation`

Industry growth alone is insufficient. The stock must demonstrate **value capture**.

## Strongest evidence

1. AI-server/data-center cooling products already span materials, 3D VC, single/two-phase cold plates, flow-control instrumentation, CDU, quick connectors, chassis and racks; multiple products are in batch delivery.
2. 2026H1 earnings growth is materially faster than revenue growth.
3. Robot joint liquid-cooling module has moved into explicit company product architecture rather than remaining an application concept.
4. The company holds a minority stake in 华科冷芯 and is integrating suspended-pump technology into systems/modules.
5. Global benchmarks VRT/NVT show that AI thermal infrastructure can translate strong demand into operating leverage and FCF when execution is good.

## Strongest counter-evidence

1. Standalone AI-liquid-cooling revenue, gross margin, backlog and customer concentration are not disclosed.
2. Broad thermal-management materials/devices gross margin was only 15.08% in 2026H1 and fell 1.62pp YoY. This prevents us from assuming that rising liquid-cooling mix is already margin-accretive.
3. H1 CFO fell 36.15% despite profit growth; inventory and receivables absorbed cash.
4. Balance sheet is leveraged rather than net-cash, so growth must eventually show cash productivity.
5. Current valuation around 54x TTM P/E requires sustained high earnings growth; recent price appreciation indicates the theme is no longer undiscovered.

## Price-implied expectation test

At approximately RMB 24.36bn market capitalization, a 15% annualized two-year shareholder return requires roughly:

- RMB 0.92bn 2028 net profit at 35x exit P/E;
- RMB 1.07bn at 30x;
- RMB 1.29bn at 25x.

Simple H1 annualization is only ~RMB 0.505bn. Therefore the current price already requires a substantial earnings-compounding path. The investment case is not “liquid cooling grows, therefore buy”; it is “can earnings and cash conversion grow fast enough relative to expectations?”

## Global benchmark interpretation

VRT and NVT are used to define **quality of conversion**, not to transfer valuation multiples mechanically into A-shares. The benchmark standard is: strong sales growth should increasingly produce operating leverage and FCF. 飞荣达 currently clears the earnings-growth gate but has not yet cleared the cash-conversion and thermal-margin gates.

## U.S. macro transmission

U.S. macro affects the case in two directions:

- **Demand:** hyperscaler AI capex, accelerator/rack deployment and higher rack power density support global liquid-cooling demand.
- **Valuation:** sticky inflation and restrictive rates raise discount rates and can compress AI-infrastructure multiples even when earnings rise.

Thus a strong industry does not guarantee a strong stock return. Earnings revision and multiple compression must be evaluated separately.

## Action logic

`WATCH` is deliberate, not indecision.

A starter review becomes justified if either:

1. Price resets to roughly RMB 35 or below while the operating thesis remains intact; or
2. New evidence establishes materially stronger AI-liquid-cooling revenue, margin and cash conversion such that a 2028 base net-profit path above roughly RMB 1.0–1.1bn becomes credible.

No robot headline alone is sufficient.

## Skill-level lesson status

No new core Skill rule is promoted from this single case. Three candidate lessons are preserved:

1. Separate the current cash-flow engine from future-option markets in emerging technology themes.
2. Rising liquid-cooling penetration does not guarantee supplier margin expansion; content, margin and cash conversion must be tracked together.
3. Cross-market leaders are useful quality benchmarks without being mechanical fair-value comparables.
