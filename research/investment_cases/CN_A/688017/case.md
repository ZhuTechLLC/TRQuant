# 绿的谐波 688017.SH — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / AVOID CHASING`.

## Decision question
Can Green Harmonic compound humanoid-robot harmonic-reducer demand fast enough to outrun an exceptionally demanding valuation after the older “TianGong exclusive supplier” narrative has been invalidated by newer multi-source evidence?

## What is established
- FY2025 revenue ~RMB571m (+47.3%), attributable NP ~RMB124m (+121.4%), CFO ~RMB152m.
- 2026Q1 revenue ~RMB140m (+42.96%), NP ~RMB32.6m (+61.17%).
- Company has batch delivery to international head humanoid customers and growing electromechanical rotary-actuator business.
- Historical TianGong supply relationship is real, but 2026 evidence indicates TianGong uses a mixed/multi-supplier transmission architecture; SanKai planetary reducers appear to supply many current units, and Laifu has direct delivery evidence.

## Thesis
The business is a genuine humanoid component winner. The stock thesis is much harder: at current capitalization, even very rapid earnings growth can be offset by multiple compression. The dominant question is not whether humanoids need reducers; it is whether Green Harmonic can capture enough unit volume/content/margin to justify the price.

## Counter-evidence
- “Exclusive TianGong supplier” should be removed from the thesis.
- Harmonic reducers are only one transmission route; high-impact/high-torque joints can adopt planetary or other architectures.
- Q1 CFO was weak despite strong earnings.
- Current valuation leaves little room for merely good execution.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 reference ~RMB314.35/share, ~RMB57.6bn market cap, roughly >400x trailing P/E. For ~15% annualized two-year return, 2028 NP would need about RMB953m at 80x or RMB1.27bn at 60x — many times FY2025 NP.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** global humanoid volumes scale extremely rapidly, Green Harmonic wins broad multi-customer share, actuators add content, margin expands and valuation remains structurally high.
- **Base:** industry grows strongly but competition/mixed architectures cap share and multiple compresses.
- **Bear:** growth remains real but below price-implied expectations; multiple normalization overwhelms EPS growth.

## Kill criteria
1. Major humanoid customers reduce harmonic content or shift architecture materially.
2. Revenue growth decelerates sharply before operating scale is achieved.
3. Margin/cash conversion deteriorates with competition.
4. Price-implied growth remains far above evidenced capacity/order conversion.
