# 奥比中光 688322.SH — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `AVOID / WATCH`.

## Decision question
Can Orbbec's strong robotics 3D-vision positioning reaccelerate revenue and produce durable operating leverage quickly enough to justify a very high valuation after 2026H1 growth and cash conversion weakened?

## What is established
- FY2025 revenue ~RMB941m (+66.66%), attributable NP ~RMB128m, first annual profit after listing.
- Company disclosures identify Gemini/Femto deployments/adaptations with multiple robot customers including Beijing “TianGong”, Zhiyuan and UBTECH.
- 2026H1 revenue ~RMB437.6m (+0.49%), NP ~RMB41.6m (-30.82%), deducted NP ~RMB23.0m (-23.67%), CFO ~RMB13.9m (-83.51%). R&D ~RMB111.4m, ~25.46% of revenue.
- Current TianGong 3.0 official architecture includes Mid-360 lidar and optional cameras; exact current camera vendor/count is not public enough to apply historical camera content mechanically.

## Thesis
Robotics vision is strategically important, but the stock requires renewed revenue acceleration and R&D operating leverage. Historical named-customer adoption establishes capability and design-in quality; it does not establish current TianGong unit content or near-term earnings growth.

## Counter-evidence
- H1 revenue essentially flat and Q2 weakened.
- Profit and CFO declined materially.
- High R&D is rational for platform leadership but increases duration risk if commercialization lags.
- Current valuation remains extreme relative to current earnings.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 reference ~RMB101/share, ~RMB41.7bn market cap, ~380x trailing P/E. At a 50x 2028 exit P/E, roughly RMB1.1bn 2028 NP would be required for ~15% annualized two-year return, versus H1 simple annualization of only ~RMB83m.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** robot shipments create a second acceleration wave; higher-volume standardized sensors lower unit cost and R&D leverage appears.
- **Base:** technology adoption grows but revenue timing is lumpy and multiple compresses.
- **Bear:** customer architectures commoditize/shift, revenue remains slow while R&D stays high and valuation resets.

## Kill criteria
1. Robot customer wins fail to translate into renewed revenue acceleration.
2. R&D stays >20% of revenue without operating leverage over multiple periods.
3. Cash conversion remains weak.
4. Current high multiple persists while estimate growth falls.
