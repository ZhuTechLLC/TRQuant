# 飞龙股份 002536.SZ — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `AVOID / WATCH`.

## Decision question
Can Feilong's real server/data-center liquid-cooling pump/valve orders produce enough profitable mix improvement to offset severe current margin and earnings deterioration, and is there any evidence that robot liquid cooling is more than an application adjacency?

## Established evidence
- 2026H1 revenue ~RMB2.206bn (+2.03%), attributable NP ~RMB75.7m (-64.02%), deducted NP ~RMB74.6m (-65.74%), CFO ~RMB125.8m (-59.5%).
- Gross margin ~20.5%, down about 5pp.
- Non-auto/new-energy and non-auto revenue grew strongly, but reported gross margin was lower than group historical economics.
- Company discloses liquid-cooling electronic pumps/thermal-control valves and some batch-supplied projects; H1 materials referenced >50,000 units of terminal orders across liquid-cooling pump platforms.
- This establishes real liquid-cooling commercialization, **not** a proven humanoid-robot liquid-cooling design-in.

## Thesis / counter-thesis
Feilong is the batch's key counterexample to “real orders = good stock”. Real liquid-cooling order growth can coexist with collapsing profit, margin and CFO. The binding variable is realized economics, not order headlines.

## Valuation
8/21 reference ~RMB46.97/share after a strong move, ~RMB27bn market cap; public trailing P/E was >100x depending provider while earnings were falling.

## Kill criteria
Current avoid state persists unless H2 shows margin recovery, profit stabilization and cash conversion. A robot-liquid-cooling label without direct customer evidence is not an upgrade trigger.
