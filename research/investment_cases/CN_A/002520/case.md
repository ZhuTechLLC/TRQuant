## Decision question
Does Rifa's machine-tool capability for processing robot-related reducer/shaft/roller-screw components create a credible equity thesis despite losses, audit/internal-control concerns and absent evidence of material robot-related orders?

## Established evidence
- FY2025 revenue ~RMB1.175bn and attributable net loss ~RMB209m.
- 2025 financial statements received a qualified audit opinion; internal-control audit included an emphasis paragraph.
- 2026Q1 revenue ~RMB115m (-67.1%), attributable NP ~RMB33.8m but deducted NP only ~RMB0.2m; CFO ~RMB18m (-80.6%).
- Company states its grinders can process shafts, RV/harmonic-reducer components and planetary roller-screw components used in humanoid robots.
- This is **capex-equipment/application capability**, not evidence of current robot BOM supply, robot orders or liquid-cooling exposure.

## Thesis / counter-thesis
There is no sufficient decision-grade robot thesis at present. The first task is operating and reporting-quality recovery. Robot-related machining is optional upside only after real orders are disclosed.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 reference ~RMB5.94/share, ~RMB4.46bn market cap; trailing earnings are loss-making and P/B was high on public data. Conventional P/E valuation is not meaningful.

## Kill criteria
The case is already in avoid state until audit/reporting issues are resolved, recurring profitability is restored and robot-related equipment orders become material and verifiable.
