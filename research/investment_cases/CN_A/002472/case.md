# 双环传动 002472.SZ — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / VALUE CHALLENGER`.

## Decision question
Can Shuanghuan's profitable auto-gear business plus its controlled RV-reducer asset (环动科技) create meaningful humanoid-robot upside after accounting for subsidiary economics and IPO dilution, without paying a pure-robot multiple for a still-small robot contribution?

## Established evidence
- FY2025 revenue RMB9.112bn (+3.77%), attributable NP RMB1.262bn (+23.21%), CFO RMB2.391bn (+42.23%).
- 2026Q1 revenue RMB2.095bn (+1.49%), NP RMB284m (+2.93%).
- 环动科技's 2025 main-business revenue was ~RMB437m, >92% RV reducers; disclosed utilization exceeded 100% on stated capacity basis.
- Exchange inquiry materials say 环动 is developing humanoid core components and has long-term co-development with an unnamed international leading enterprise.
- This does not establish current TianGong supply.

## Thesis / counter-thesis
This is a financially stronger **value challenger** to high-multiple robot names. The key is value capture: robot/RV growth occurs inside a subsidiary whose ownership may be diluted through IPO. Group investors must underwrite incremental contribution, not just subsidiary industry status.

## Valuation
8/21 reference ~RMB35.86/share, ~RMB30.5bn market cap, ~24x trailing P/E. Much less demanding than most robot-theme peers, but robot upside remains a minority option.

## Kill criteria
1. 环动 humanoid programs remain prototype-level without batch revenue.
2. Subsidiary dilution/value leakage offsets growth economics.
3. Auto-gear cash engine weakens materially.
4. Market re-rates the parent as a pure humanoid stock before group EPS contribution appears.
