# Cross-case lessons — CN A-share robotics & liquid cooling — 2026-08-23

These are **candidate lessons**, not standing Skill rules. Promotion requires additional cases and counterexamples across sectors/markets.

### Evidence-stage ladder before theme promotion
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Cases:** 002979, 301252, 600580, 002896, 002520
- **Observation:** Companies occupy materially different stages from capability to batch revenue.
- **Reusable principle:** Preserve the ladder `capability -> cooperation/design-in -> validation -> small batch -> stable batch -> material revenue -> margin -> CFO/FCF -> EPS revision`; do not skip stages in narrative or valuation.
- **Why it may work:** Each step removes a different technical, customer, scale, pricing or cash-conversion uncertainty.
- **Counterexamples/limits:** Some programs can move very quickly through stages; disclosure may lag real production.
- **Promotion evidence needed:** Apply across semiconductors, data-center infrastructure, auto parts and biotech commercialization analogs.

### Theme purity is not stock attractiveness
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Cases:** 688017, 688322 vs 002472, 601689, 002979
- **Observation:** Pure/high-visibility robotics names can have better industrial positioning but far worse reverse-valuation hurdles.
- **Reusable principle:** Separate industry winner status from security attractiveness; always bridge price to required future earnings/cash flow.
- **Why it may work:** Shareholder return depends on expectations paid for, not only business growth.
- **Counterexamples/limits:** Exceptional compounders can sustain premium multiples longer than standard normalization assumptions.
- **Promotion evidence needed:** Cross-market winners with both successful and failed premium valuations.

### Real orders do not guarantee shareholder economics
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Cases:** 002536; contrasted with 300602 and global VRT/NVT benchmarks
- **Observation:** Feilong shows real liquid-cooling order progress alongside sharply weaker profit, margin and CFO.
- **Reusable principle:** Force the conversion chain `orders -> deliveries -> recognized revenue -> gross margin -> operating profit -> working capital -> CFO/FCF` before calling a theme financially validated.
- **Why it may work:** Low-margin mix, pricing, launch costs and working capital can absorb headline growth.
- **Counterexamples/limits:** Early ramp periods can rationally show temporary margin/CFO pressure before later scale benefits.
- **Promotion evidence needed:** Test multiple high-growth component ramps over full cycles.

### Platform diversification reduces technical-route risk but can dilute EPS sensitivity
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Cases:** 002600
- **Observation:** Lingyi participates across structures, thermal, modules, ODM and multiple robot platforms, reducing single-BOM risk, but its RMB50bn+ revenue base makes one program insufficient to transform group earnings.
- **Reusable principle:** Underwrite both **content breadth** and **group-base dilution** for large manufacturing platforms.
- **Counterexamples/limits:** A new high-margin platform can still become material rapidly if content value and customer count scale together.
- **Promotion evidence needed:** Test against large EMS, auto-tier and semiconductor platform suppliers.

### Static supply-chain maps need model/year/component keys
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Cases:** TianGong 2025-2026; 688017 historical narrative; SanKai/Laifu/ENCOS evidence
- **Observation:** TianGong's transmission and joint supplier evidence changed across generations; older “exclusive” claims became obsolete.
- **Reusable principle:** Store load-bearing BOM claims as `manufacturer + model + year/generation + component + evidence date`, not as permanent company-to-brand edges.
- **Counterexamples/limits:** Some long-cycle certified suppliers can remain stable for many years.
- **Promotion evidence needed:** Validate across multiple robot OEM generations and other fast-iterating hardware industries.

### Adjust subsidiary theme exposure for parent ownership and dilution
- **Status:** Candidate
- **Cases:** 002472 / 环动科技
- **Observation:** A parent can own a high-quality robotics asset whose growth economics are diluted by minority ownership or IPO issuance.
- **Reusable principle:** Translate subsidiary revenue/profit into parent attributable economics before assigning theme value to the parent security.
- **Counterexamples/limits:** Strategic control, synergies or market value of the retained stake can still create material parent value.
- **Promotion evidence needed:** Test spin-offs and listed-subsidiary cases across sectors.
