## Decision question
Does Estun's genuine industrial-robot turnaround and early embodied-intelligence work justify the current valuation before humanoid commercialization becomes material and after separating recurring operating improvement from fair-value gains?

## Established evidence
- FY2025 revenue RMB4.888bn (+21.93%), attributable NP RMB45m versus a large prior-year loss, deducted NP only ~RMB7.3m, CFO ~RMB507m.
- 2026Q1 revenue RMB1.217bn (-2.22%), NP RMB97.8m (+674.6%), deduct NP RMB19.4m, CFO -RMB147m.
- 2026H1 headline NP ~RMB161m reportedly included roughly RMB99.5m fair-value gain; deducted NP ~RMB70m, so headline growth overstates recurring improvement.
- Company is developing embodied/AI robots; Codroid02 was described in small-batch validation. Industrial-robot leadership is real, but current humanoid revenue is not established as material.

## Thesis / counter-thesis
Estun is not a fake robotics company; the error would be treating industrial-robot scale as automatic humanoid earnings. The investment case needs recurring operating-margin/cash improvement and evidence that humanoid programs move beyond small-batch validation.

## Valuation
8/21 reference ~RMB33.57/share, ~RMB32.5bn market cap, public trailing P/E around ~250x because recurring earnings remain small.

## Kill criteria
1. Recurring deduct-profit/cash conversion fails to improve.
2. Humanoid products remain validation projects without material orders.
3. Headline NP depends materially on nonrecurring fair-value gains.
4. Valuation stays extreme without core earnings scale.
