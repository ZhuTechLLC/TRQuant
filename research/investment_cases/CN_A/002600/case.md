# 领益智造 002600.SZ — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / EVENT-READY` — default new capital 0 pending H1 re-underwrite.

## Decision question
Can Lingyi convert its multi-platform manufacturing position in AI hardware and humanoid robotics into enough higher-value thermal, joint/module and ODM content to reaccelerate group earnings and cash flow, rather than merely adding low-margin assembly revenue to a RMB50bn+ revenue base?

## What is established
- FY2025 revenue RMB51.429bn (+16.2%), attributable NP RMB2.288bn (+30.34%), CFO RMB4.433bn (+10.4%).
- 2026Q1 revenue RMB12.643bn (+9.99%), attributable NP RMB392m (-30.7%), deducted NP RMB239m (-33.15%), CFO RMB792m (-2.63%).
- Readore/立敏达 adds UQD, manifold, liquid-cooling plates, phase-change liquid cooling and VC/3DVC capability; company says products are batch-delivered to North American compute customers.
- In robotics, company says it has completed thousands of sets of hardware/whole-machine assembly services for multiple leading embodied-intelligence customers; TianGong Ultra/3.0 entered batch production in the Beijing superfactory.
- For Honor marathon champion robot “闪电”, Lingyi disclosed batch delivery of 159 types of core metal structural parts/surface-treatment components covering hip, leg, foot and torso.

## Thesis
The attractive thesis is **not** “TianGong order volume alone”. It is platform content expansion:
`structure -> thermal -> joint/module -> charging -> ODM/test -> multi-customer replication`.
The large group revenue base lowers single-project EPS sensitivity, but multi-client content expansion can lower customer and technical-route risk.

## Counter-evidence
- 2026Q1 profit contracted despite revenue growth.
- New robot/AI hardware revenue, gross margin and customer concentration are not separately disclosed.
- Capacity plans and factory throughput are not orders or recognized revenue.
- Current TianGong liquid-cooling BOM is not sufficiently public to attribute the complete cooling system to Lingyi.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 reference: ~RMB12.24/share, ~RMB99bn market cap, roughly mid-40s TTM P/E depending data provider. A reverse check implies that at a 30x 2028 exit multiple, roughly RMB4.4bn 2028 NP would be needed for ~15% annualized two-year equity return; at 25x, roughly RMB5.3bn. This is an analytical hurdle, not a forecast.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** H1/H2 earnings reaccelerate; Readore AI liquid-cooling grows rapidly; robot content expands from assembly/structure into thermal/joints/modules across multiple customers; cash conversion remains healthy.
- **Base:** new businesses grow but are diluted by legacy scale; margins recover only gradually; stock remains dependent on multiple support.
- **Bear:** robot/AI headlines do not translate into material group profit, margin pressure persists, or cash conversion weakens while valuation remains elevated.

## Kill criteria
1. Multi-quarter group margin/earnings deterioration with no credible new-business operating leverage.
2. New-business growth remains mostly assembly/low-value content and fails to move profit mix.
3. Meaningful customer/design losses in AI thermal or robotics.
4. Capex/working-capital intensity rises without revenue, margin and CFO conversion.
