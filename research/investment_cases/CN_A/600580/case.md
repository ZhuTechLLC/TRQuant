**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`

## Decision question
Can Wolong convert genuine frameless-motor, joint-module and liquid-cooling R&D capability plus robot-customer cooperation into material recurring robot earnings, rather than remaining primarily an industrial-motor company with thematic optionality?

## Established evidence
- FY2025 revenue ~RMB15.45bn, attributable NP ~RMB1.13bn (+42%), deducted NP ~RMB823m (+29.6%), CFO ~RMB1.78bn (+16%).
- 2026Q1 revenue ~RMB3.99bn (-1.3%), NP ~RMB276m (+2.9%), deducted NP ~RMB217m (-7.6%), CFO ~RMB257m (-15.5%).
- Company materials describe high-burst joint modules, servo drives, frameless torque motors and a liquid-cooling dedicated water-cooling technology platform; public cooperation references include Zhiyuan, Siasun-related entities and Lenovo.
- A circulated claim that Readore/立敏达 is a Wolong subsidiary is false; Readore belongs to Lingyi.

## Thesis / counter-thesis
Wolong has real electromechanical capability and real robot development activity. The unresolved issue is economic materiality. It should not receive the same evidence grade as companies already disclosing mass robot-component shipments or robot thermal small-batch deliveries.

## Valuation
8/21 reference ~RMB30.92/share, ~RMB48.3bn market cap, roughly low-40s trailing P/E on public data; deducted-earnings multiple is higher.

## Kill criteria
1. Robot cooperation fails to generate disclosed batch revenue/orders.
2. Core industrial-motor earnings weaken while robot capex/R&D rises.
3. Liquid-cooling capability remains R&D rather than customer productization.
4. Valuation rerates on incorrect affiliate/customer narratives.
