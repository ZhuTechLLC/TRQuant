# 同星科技 301252.SZ — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / THESIS-BUILDING`.

## Decision question
Can Tongxing turn early robot precision-structure batch supply and robot thermal-module small-batch deliveries into a high-margin second growth curve before current valuation outruns earnings and cash conversion?

## What is established
- 2026H1 revenue RMB783.3m (+26.25%).
- Attributable NP RMB51.24m (-25.50%); deducted NP RMB48.63m (-9.21%).
- CFO -RMB67.87m; gross margin ~17.44%, down ~2.43pp.
- Through Wuxi Tongwei, robot body/joint/reducer precision structural parts have entered supply lists and stable batch supply.
- Robot thermal modules for head compute chips, chest electronics, joint motors and dexterous hands progressed from prototype/bench/whole-machine validation to small-batch deliveries.

## Thesis
Tongxing is one of the cleaner public examples of **robot thermal-management productization** rather than mere technology capability. The attraction is small-company revenue sensitivity if small-batch programs scale. The problem is that financial conversion currently lags the industrial narrative.

## Counter-evidence
- Profit declined despite revenue growth.
- Gross margin and cash conversion weakened.
- Named robot customers, robot revenue, robot thermal ASP/backlog and margins are undisclosed.
- Data-center liquid cooling and robot thermal management must not be aggregated as one revenue pool without evidence.

## Valuation / expectations
8/20-8/21 reference ~RMB32.9/share and ~RMB5.6bn market cap, around ~60x trailing earnings depending provider. For ~15% annualized two-year return at 30x 2028 P/E, 2028 NP would need roughly RMB245m versus H1 simple annualization ~RMB102m.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** robot thermal/precision orders scale rapidly, mix raises gross margin and CFO turns positive.
- **Base:** robot products grow from a small base but legacy mix and investment keep margins modest.
- **Bear:** design-ins remain low-volume, price competition persists, working-capital absorbs growth.

## Kill criteria
1. Robot thermal programs fail to progress beyond small batch.
2. Gross margin keeps declining despite mix shift.
3. CFO remains structurally negative while receivables/inventory grow.
4. Valuation expands without corresponding EPS revisions.
