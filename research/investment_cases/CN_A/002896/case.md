## Decision question
Is there enough current primary evidence of humanoid-robot design-ins and batch economics to justify an extreme valuation despite declining profit and cash conversion?

## Established evidence
- FY2025 revenue RMB1.041bn (+6.61%), attributable NP RMB62.7m (-13.57%), deducted NP RMB46.5m (-20.27%), CFO RMB30.7m (-85.3%).
- 2026Q1 revenue RMB242m (+5.12%), NP RMB11.1m (-36.42%), deducted NP RMB6.8m (-43.19%), CFO negative.
- Company has genuine precision reducers, reducer motors and intelligent execution units.
- A historical 2023 direct IR response said the company had not contacted humanoid robot companies at that time. Current popular claims of binding Unitree/Zhiyuan/Tesla were not established by strong primary evidence in this review.

## Thesis / counter-thesis
Capability is real; current investable humanoid exposure is **not sufficiently evidenced**. With deteriorating earnings and a very high valuation, the burden of proof is on current design-in/batch revenue, not on product adjacency.

## Valuation
8/21 reference ~RMB68.62/share, ~RMB13.5bn market cap, >200x trailing P/E on public provider data.

## Kill criteria
Already in avoid state unless: named/credible customer evidence, batch revenue, margin improvement and cash conversion emerge. A further multiple expansion without those data strengthens the avoid conclusion.
