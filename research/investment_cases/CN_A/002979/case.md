# 雷赛智能 002979.SZ — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / START-SMALL REVIEW AFTER H1 ACTUAL`.

## Decision question
Can Leadshine convert unusually broad humanoid-robot design-ins into sustained high-volume motors, joint modules and dexterous-hand economics while preserving gross margin and cash conversion, fast enough to justify a roughly high-50s earnings multiple on current reconstructed earnings?

## What is established
- FY2025 revenue RMB1.874bn (+18.28%); attributable NP RMB225m (+12.42%); CFO RMB285m (+34.88%).
- 2025 frameless torque-motor deliveries exceeded 120,000 units, >20x growth; company says robot core products entered 80% of mainstream domestic humanoid makers' supply systems.
- Planetary/harmonic joint modules and high-DOF dexterous hands achieved batch supply; company reported orders from dozens of mainstream robot customers and is building 2m-unit annual automated capacity.
- Corrected 2026H1 preannouncement: revenue RMB1.230–1.266bn (+38–42%), attributable NP RMB184–196m (+55–65%), deduct NP +55–65%.

## Thesis
Leadshine is a direct **execution-layer platform** rather than a single robot-BOM story. Customer breadth plus motor/encoder/drive/module/hand content can produce both volume and content-value expansion. The strongest evidence in the current A-share robot set is the combination of real shipments and accelerating company-level earnings.

## Counter-evidence
- Direct TianGong supply has not been established.
- Robot segment standalone revenue and margin are not publicly isolated.
- 2m-unit capacity is a plan, not utilization or orders.
- Share-based compensation is meaningful; adjusted figures must not replace GAAP earnings.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 reference ~RMB55.42/share and ~RMB17.5bn market cap. Using a reconstructed trailing earnings bridge gives ~high-50s P/E. For a ~15% annualized two-year return, 2028 NP would need roughly RMB579m at 40x or RMB772m at 30x. H1 midpoint simple annualization is ~RMB381m, so the hurdle is demanding but more plausible than many high-valuation robot peers if scale-up continues.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** H1 actual confirms preannouncement, robot mix rises, capacity utilization scales, gross margin holds and CFO follows earnings.
- **Base:** robot growth is strong but still a minority of company economics; broader automation recovery contributes; valuation remains demanding.
- **Bear:** customer programs fragment, price competition compresses margin, capacity runs below expectations, or growth normalizes before earnings catch valuation.

## Kill criteria
1. Material deterioration of robot shipment/order breadth across multiple customers.
2. Gross-margin erosion despite volume growth.
3. Capacity expansion without utilization/cash conversion.
4. Company-level earnings revisions fall materially while valuation remains elevated.
