# CN_A Robotics & Liquid Cooling Cross-Case Comparison — 2026Q3

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Purpose:** Compare evidence quality, financial conversion and price-implied expectations across robotics / AI-liquid-cooling candidates without collapsing them into a mechanical score.

## Evidence ladder used
`capability -> named cooperation/design-in -> sample/validation -> small batch -> stable batch -> material revenue -> margin contribution -> CFO/FCF -> EPS revision`

A company can be strong at one stage and weak at the next. The comparison therefore separates **industry validation**, **financial conversion**, **valuation burden**, and **evidence directness**.

## Cross-case conclusions

1. **Theme purity is not stock attractiveness.** The purest robotics/LC names often carry the highest price-implied earnings burden.
2. **Evidence stage matters.** “Can make” and “partner” are materially weaker than stable batch, recognized revenue and cash conversion.
3. **Real orders are not sufficient.** 飞龙 demonstrates that liquid-cooling orders can grow while margin, NP and CFO deteriorate.
4. **Platform suppliers have two-sided economics.** 领益 reduces customer/architecture risk through multi-platform manufacturing, but a large group base dilutes single-program EPS sensitivity.
5. **Static BOM maps decay quickly.** TianGong 2025 and 2026 supplier/architecture evidence differs; cases must be keyed by model/year/component when claims are load-bearing.
6. **Ownership matters.** 双环's attractive RV/humanoid exposure resides partly in 环动科技; parent shareholders capture only ownership-adjusted economics, especially after IPO dilution.
