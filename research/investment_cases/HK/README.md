# TRQuant HK Investment Cases

Hong Kong-listed securities are stored separately from `CN_A` and `US` because the same operating company can have materially different price, liquidity, investor base, shorting/derivatives access, currency exposure and valuation in Hong Kong.

For A+H issuers, operating evidence may be shared by reference, but valuation, price-implied expectations, action state and decision records are security-specific. Do not copy an A-share action into the H-share case mechanically.
