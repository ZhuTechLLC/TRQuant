# 优必选 09880.HK — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `WATCH / EVENT-READY` for 2026-08-28 interim results.

## Decision question
Can UBTECH convert early industrial/full-size humanoid commercialization into sustained gross-profit growth and a credible path to cash breakeven fast enough to justify a ~HKD36.8bn equity value, while maintaining customer/order quality and controlling R&D/capital needs?

## Established evidence
- FY2025 revenue RMB2.001bn (+53.3%).
- Full-size embodied-intelligence humanoid products/services revenue RMB820.6m, +2,203.7%, becoming the largest revenue source.
- Gross profit RMB753.8m; gross margin improved to 37.7% from 28.7%.
- Net loss narrowed to RMB789.8m from RMB1.160bn.
- UBTECH and Beijing Humanoid Innovation Center jointly released Walker Tienkung; annual report states starting price RMB299k and positions UBTECH as an initiating/general-managing entity of the center.
- Company says Walker S2 entered mass production/delivery in 2025, but profitability and cash conversion remain the central investment questions.

## Thesis
UBTECH has one of the strongest direct public revenue bridges from humanoid robots to a listed company. It is fundamentally different from component suppliers whose robot revenue is still undisclosed. However, revenue growth is not yet shareholder cash flow: the company remains loss-making and valuation must be underwritten on future gross profit/operating leverage, not current P/E.

## Valuation / expectations
8/21 close HKD83.90, equity value ~HKD36.8bn, 52-week high HKD161. At current scale, market value is many times FY2025 revenue and requires continued high humanoid growth plus large loss narrowing. Simple P/E is not meaningful.

## Event readiness for Aug 28
Key questions:
1. H1 humanoid revenue and delivery growth versus FY2025 exit rate.
2. Gross margin by business and whether humanoid mix is accretive.
3. Operating loss, R&D, SBC and cash burn.
4. Cash/balance-sheet runway and any financing/dilution need.
5. Walker S/Tienkung orders, customer concentration and collections.
6. 2026 revenue/delivery guidance and overseas market-access commentary.

## Kill criteria
1. Humanoid revenue growth decelerates sharply or depends on low-quality/nonrepeat orders.
2. Gross margin falls materially as deliveries scale.
3. Cash burn/dilution path worsens without credible breakeven trajectory.
4. Customer concentration/receivables or order cancellation risk rises materially.
