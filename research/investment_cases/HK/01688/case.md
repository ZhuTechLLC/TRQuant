## Why a separate case
01688.HK and 002600.SZ represent the same operating company but not the same security opportunity. The H share trades in HKD, has different liquidity/investor positioning and currently trades at a very large A/H price discount.

## Operating thesis
Use the same operating evidence as the A-share case: FY2025 revenue RMB51.429bn and attributable NP RMB2.288bn; Q1 2026 profit pressure; Readore AI liquid-cooling expansion; TianGong scale manufacturing; Honor robot structural-component batch delivery; multi-customer robot hardware/ODM capability.

The key operating question remains whether AI/robot content expansion improves **group margin and EPS**, not whether the company has robotics exposure.

## Security-specific valuation asymmetry
- H-share IPO price: HKD10.18; listing date 2026-06-26.
- 2026-08-21 H close: HKD6.24, ~38.7% below IPO price.
- Same-date A-share reference: ~CNY12.24.
- 2026-08-21 HKD/CNY ~0.857, so HKD6.24 ≈ CNY5.35 per H share. On a simple per-share currency-adjusted basis, the H share traded at only ~44% of the A-share price, i.e. ~56% A/H discount.

This is not risk-free arbitrage: A/H shares are not freely fungible for ordinary investors and the investor/liquidity/flow structures differ. But it materially lowers the price-implied operating hurdle for 01688.HK relative to 002600.SZ.

## Counter-evidence / risks

- The H share is a recent IPO with limited trading history and possible supply/positioning effects.
- A/H discount can persist for structural reasons.
- New-business segment economics remain undisclosed.

## Action logic
The underlying business is currently only `WATCH / EVENT-READY` on the A share. The H share's large valuation discount is enough to justify a **START-SMALL REVIEW**, but not enough to override earnings evidence. A small position would require H1 thesis intact, acceptable liquidity/portfolio concentration and no new negative cash/margin evidence.

2. AI/robot new-business content fails to improve mix/profit.
3. H discount narrows materially without corresponding earnings upgrades.
