**Opportunity setup:** post-earnings revision continuation candidate — provisional, not a validated trading rule

## Executive conclusion

This review separates three questions that had become conflated after Heartflow's Q2 2026 report:

1. **Was the scanner right to escalate HTFL for research?** Yes. The Q2 evidence was materially stronger than the pre-event public baseline.
2. **Was `New Ignition` the correct lifecycle label after the +35.7% earnings gap?** No. Heartflow already had a scaled FFRCT franchise and the post-gap economic diluted valuation required a later-stage classification.
3. **Did the lifecycle correction imply that the opportunity itself was false?** No. `False New Ignition` does not imply `False Opportunity`. A Confirmation / Early Momentum stock can still present a valid opportunity setup, but that setup must be underwritten separately from the long-duration fundamental case.

The durable methodological update is therefore:

`lifecycle classification != opportunity setup`

This does **not** establish a buy rule. It establishes a research architecture: after a material event, re-underwrite the company lifecycle and the current opportunity on separate axes.

## 3. Lifecycle versus opportunity capture

### Lifecycle conclusion

HTFL remains:

**Confirmation / Early Momentum / Second Product S-Curve**

It is not restored to strict New Ignition merely because the stock continued higher on 8/18.

### Opportunity conclusion

The post-Q2 sequence is nevertheless research-relevant:

`major fundamental revision -> +35.7% event gap -> gap largely held on 8/17 -> new high / higher close on 8/18`

This is sufficient to classify a **provisional post-earnings revision continuation setup for study**. It is not sufficient to claim a general edge or an automatic add signal.

The correct architecture is to maintain two independent axes:

- **Company/economic lifecycle:** Discovery / Confirmation / Momentum / Harvest / Reset / Re-ignition.
- **Current opportunity setup:** fundamental mispricing / event-confirmation / continuation / pullback / failed-breakout / no-action.

A candidate can fail the New Ignition lifecycle gate but remain high priority in the Opportunity Capture layer.

## 4. Current unresolved evidence gaps

The case deliberately remains `research_complete`, not `decision_grade`, because the following are still unresolved or incomplete:

1. **PIT sell-side estimate revision:** FY2026/FY2027/FY2028 consensus before and after Q2.
2. **True NTM / FY2027 / FY2028 valuation:** consensus denominators, not analyst-created inference.
3. **Float + short + options + flow:** current float definition, settlement-date short interest, borrow/utilization where available, option-chain volume/OI/IV and any defensible flow evidence.
4. **Post-earnings historical analogs:** PIT event study controlling for IPO age, market cap, revenue growth, beat magnitude, guide revision, valuation, gap size, close location, relative volume and market regime.
5. **Product-level operating bridge:** FFRCT standalone revenue/volume, Plaque analysis volume, attach/utilization, overlap with FFRCT, unique CCTA denominator and product-level realized ASP.
6. **True microstructure:** intraday VWAP / anchored VWAP, trade-size distribution and price-volume acceptance if direct data are available.
7. **Competitive commercial validation:** actual account wins/losses, renewals, paid cases and pricing evidence rather than product-description comparisons.
8. **Verified historical user transaction/sizing record:** none recovered, so the case does not invent one.

## 5. Candidate lessons for Skill evolution

### Candidate lesson A — False New Ignition != False Opportunity

**Observation:** HTFL's post-Q2 strict New Ignition label was incorrect, yet the stock showed additional price follow-through after the lifecycle correction.

**Reusable candidate principle:** lifecycle classification and opportunity capture must be evaluated on separate axes. Rejecting a New Ignition label should not automatically remove a strong estimate-revision / price-acceptance candidate from the active attention queue.

**Counterexample / limitation:** post-event continuation can fail after overreaction. The HTFL path alone does not prove PEAD, momentum continuation or a profitable entry rule.

**Promotion evidence needed:** a PIT event study across comparable large beat + guide-raise gaps, with valuation, growth, IPO age, gap size, relative volume and market-regime controls.

### Candidate lesson B — Gross-margin leverage != Operating leverage

**Observation:** HTFL Q2 GAAP gross margin expanded materially while GAAP OpEx grew faster than revenue and GAAP operating loss widened YoY.

**Reusable candidate principle:** do not promote a margin story to `operating leverage` from gross margin alone. Require operating-expense growth, operating margin and eventually FCF/share evidence.

**Promotion evidence needed:** validation across additional software/medtech growth cases and subsequent HTFL quarters.

### Supported implementation guardrail — use economic diluted valuation after rerating

For post-IPO / high-SBC growth candidates, current mathematical room must be tested using a clearly defined **economic diluted equity value and EV convention**, not basic market cap alone. The share convention, option treatment, cash/debt date and lease convention must be explicit.

## 6. Decision implication

The business thesis remains constructive. The completed 8/18 session strengthens evidence that the market continued to process the Q2 revision, but the higher price also raises the valuation hurdle to roughly 16.6x FY2026 revenue on the reconstructed ex-lease EV convention.

Therefore:

- do not downgrade the business because valuation is high;
- do not call the stock New Ignition because it is strong;
- do not convert follow-through into an automatic buy rule;
- keep the stock in the active case / opportunity-capture queue;
- require either better forward-estimate evidence, better price asymmetry, or empirically validated event-continuation evidence before promoting the setup into a reusable execution rule.
