## Executive conclusion

The Q2 2026 event produced a real and material sell-side estimate revision, but the stock rerating has been much larger than the forward-revenue revision.

Before Q2, public pre-earnings consensus was approximately $56.64M for Q2 revenue, $230.07M for FY2026 revenue, and $283.22M for FY2027 revenue. Heartflow reported $64.082M of Q2 revenue and raised its own FY2026 revenue guidance to $246M-$250M. After the report, S&P Global Market Intelligence consensus displayed by StockAnalysis was approximately $248.94M for FY2026 and $309.23M for FY2027 as of 2026-08-17.

This implies an approximately +8.2% revision to FY2026 consensus and +9.2% revision to FY2027 consensus versus the pre-Q2 baseline. Over the same interval, HTFL rose from the 2026-08-13 close of $31.01 to the 2026-08-19 close of $45.53, approximately +46.8%.

Using one consistent economic-dilution convention and the same Q2 balance-sheet inputs, ex-lease EV rose from approximately $2.711B at $31.01 to approximately $4.143B at $45.53. EV/FY2027 consensus therefore rose from roughly 9.6x using the pre-Q2 FY2027 consensus to roughly 13.4x using the post-Q2 FY2027 consensus. This is an approximately 40% expansion in the forward EV/Sales multiple. It is an arithmetic expectation bridge, not a causal decomposition of buyers or flows.

**Decision implication:** Q2 genuinely improved the business and forward estimates, but the post-event price now capitalizes substantially more than the near-term estimate revision. The opportunity remains active because price acceptance and fundamental revision are both strong, but the setup has migrated from `event confirmation` toward `continuation watch`. For new capital, the default state is now **Watch**, not chase/add. For an already-established small starter position, **Hold / re-underwrite** is more consistent than adding solely because the gap held.

## 8. Action state

**New capital:** `WATCH`.

Do not add solely because the gap held or because the stock made a new post-event high. At the current price, the business thesis remains stronger than before Q2, but the market has also raised both estimates and the multiple materially.

**If a small starter position already exists:** `HOLD / RE-UNDERWRITE`, not automatic add.

A future add should be justified by at least one of three independent improvements:

1. **Estimate path improves:** FY2027/FY2028 revenue, margin or FCF expectations rise enough to restore attractive forward asymmetry.
2. **Price asymmetry improves:** price falls without a corresponding deterioration in the business/expectation thesis, lowering economic diluted EV relative to the validated forward path.
3. **Opportunity edge is validated:** a PIT historical event study demonstrates a repeatable positive-expectancy continuation setup for comparable beat + guidance-raise events.

## 9. Historical-validation specification

Do not convert HTFL's path into a general trading rule until a PIT event study is completed.

Minimum event schema:

- U.S. common equity.
- Pre-event market capitalization and economic dilution captured PIT.
- Quarterly revenue growth >=25%.
- Revenue beat versus PIT consensus captured explicitly.
- Full-year guidance midpoint revision captured explicitly.
- Event-day gap and close-to-close return.
- Relative volume and close-location metrics.
- IPO age / prior lifecycle state.
- Pre-event and post-event forward revenue consensus.
- Pre-event and post-event EV/Sales.
- Market/sector regime.

Required outcome windows:

- +1, +3, +5, +10 and +20 trading sessions.
- Max favorable excursion and max adverse excursion.
- Gap-retention / failure rate.
- Relative return versus broad market and relevant sector.

Stratify results by valuation, growth, beat size, guidance revision size, event-gap size and lifecycle state. Preserve the information set available at each event date; do not use later estimate history as if it had been known contemporaneously.
