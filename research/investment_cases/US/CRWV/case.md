# CRWV — CoreWeave Golden Case

**Case ID:** US-CRWV-2025-2026  
**Status:** research_complete  
**As of:** 2026-08-16  
**Archetype:** capital-intensive hypergrowth / valuation expansion / financing dependency

## Decision summary

CoreWeave is a genuine hypergrowth AI-infrastructure company, but the investment case cannot be underwritten from revenue or adjusted EBITDA alone. The dominant question is whether contracted demand converts into shareholder cash flow after depreciation, interest, capex, lease/debt service, and dilution.

The 2025 entry near the mid-$150s is the anchor for this case. The business thesis was directionally strong, yet the stock had already moved from discovery into confirmation/momentum and carried a very demanding valuation. The case therefore separates three judgments that must never be collapsed: **business quality**, **valuation/expectations**, and **trade setup**.

## What was knowable in Q1 2025

On 2025-05-14 CoreWeave reported Q1 revenue of $981.632M, up 420% year over year, operating loss of $27.470M, net interest expense of $263.835M, revenue backlog of $25.9B, active power of about 420MW, and contracted power of about 1.6GW. The company defined backlog as RPO plus other amounts expected from committed customer contracts, subject to delivery and service-availability requirements.

The attractive narrative was real: extraordinary AI demand, large customer commitments, rapid infrastructure deployment, and a very high adjusted EBITDA margin. The offsetting evidence was also already visible: the operating business remained loss-making on a GAAP basis, interest burden was large, the model required continued financing, and backlog did not equal recognized revenue or cash.

## 2025-06 valuation reconstruction

For the 2025-06-16 checkpoint, this case uses an **analytical** diluted-share convention of about 549M shares, net debt of about $7.44B, FY2025 forward revenue of about $5.0B, and FY2025 adjusted operating income of about $815M. At a price of $158.50 this implies roughly:

- Equity value: about $87.0B
- Enterprise value: about $94.5B
- Forward EV/Sales: about **18.9x**
- EV / Adjusted Operating Income: about **115.9x**

These are not company-reported multiples. They are a reproducible research reconstruction and must retain the assumptions used. Exact values move with the diluted-share and debt convention.

The key inference is not that any one multiple makes the stock automatically uninvestable. It is that by mid-June 2025 a large portion of future success had already been capitalized into the equity price while capital conversion remained unproven.

## 2026 Q2 re-underwrite

Q2 2026 strongly confirmed demand and scale. Revenue reached $2.575B, adjusted EBITDA $1.510B, adjusted operating income $128M, net interest expense $640M, and revenue backlog approximately $104B. Active power reached 1.5GW and contracted power about 3.7GW.

The balance sheet simultaneously expanded to $46.736B of net property and equipment, $35.068B of financial debt, $16.319B of operating lease liabilities, and $5.024B of stockholders' equity at 2026-06-30.

This validates the core lesson: **growth was real, but the capital chain grew with it**.

## Dominant value-driver chain

`contracted demand -> financing capacity -> active capacity -> utilization -> revenue -> operating profit -> interest -> capex -> debt/leases -> dilution -> shareholder FCF`

The weakest and most decision-relevant links are capital cost, deployment efficiency, and eventual equity cash conversion.

## Case lessons

1. **Good business != good entry price.** Strong evidence can increase thesis confidence while a large rerating simultaneously reduces expected return.
2. **Revenue growth != shareholder return.** For capital-intensive hypergrowth, force the full conversion chain to shareholder FCF.
3. **Financing availability != cheap financing.** Access to debt can reduce execution risk while still burdening equity economics.
4. **Backlog != revenue != cash.** Contract value must be translated through delivery, availability, utilization, recognized revenue, margin, and cash collection.
5. **Momentum trade != permanent fundamental holding.** If the original entry is trend-driven, exit rules must remain explicit; do not allow strategy drift after the trend fails.
