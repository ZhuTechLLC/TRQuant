# CRWV Q2 2026 Re-underwrite

**Review date:** 2026-08-16  
**Case:** US-CRWV-2025-2026  
**Review type:** catalyst re-underwrite

## Prior state

The original bull narrative centered on extraordinary AI demand, large customer commitments, rapid infrastructure deployment, and high adjusted EBITDA margins. By mid-2025, however, valuation expansion and financing economics had become at least as important as the growth narrative.

## New evidence

CoreWeave reported Q2 2026 revenue of $2.575B, adjusted EBITDA of $1.510B, adjusted operating income of $128M, net interest expense of $640M, and revenue backlog of approximately $104B. Active power reached 1.5GW and contracted power approximately 3.7GW.

At 2026-06-30, the company reported $46.736B of net property and equipment, $35.068B of financial debt, $16.319B of operating lease liabilities, $0.221B of finance lease liabilities, and $5.024B of stockholders' equity.

## Interpretation

The demand thesis strengthened. The equity thesis did not simplify.

The dominant chain remains:

`contracted demand -> financing -> active capacity -> utilization -> revenue -> operating profit -> interest -> capex -> debt/leases -> dilution -> shareholder FCF`

The critical question is no longer whether AI demand exists. It is whether deployment economics and financing costs allow sufficiently attractive returns to accrue to common equity.

## Process diagnosis

- The 2025 case was not primarily an industry-direction error.
- The most important research gap was incomplete capital-structure and valuation underwriting.
- Revenue/adjusted EBITDA were more visible than operating profit, interest, capex, debt/lease growth, and dilution.
- A trend-driven entry near a stretched valuation would require explicit momentum exit rules; otherwise strategy drift can turn a tactical trade into an undocumented long-term hold.

## Candidate lessons

1. Force a shareholder-cash-conversion bridge for capital-intensive hypergrowth.
2. Separate financing availability from financing cost.
3. Treat multiple expansion as a change in the investment state, not as proof that the thesis became safer.
4. Preserve original trade style and invalidation rules after entry.

## Promotion decision

Keep case status at `research_complete`. Promote to `decision_grade` only after deterministic revalidation of the 2025 valuation convention and explicit review approval.
