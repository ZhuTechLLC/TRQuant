## Founder-level decision summary

TSM is not a discovery-stage AI trade. It is a proven, capital-intensive structural compounder whose shareholder-return question has migrated from **whether AI demand is real** to **whether AI/HPC demand, leading-edge process leadership, advanced packaging and high utilization can continue to grow earnings faster than already-elevated market expectations while absorbing very large capex and overseas-fab dilution**.

The most valuable historical lesson is process-related. The earliest recovered user-side TSM research record is 2025-08-19, when TSM was already inside a broader semiconductor/infrastructure watch universe. No contemporaneous buy, add, trim, stop, target or position-sizing record was recovered, and the connected IBKR account shows no TSM trades in the available 2025 Q3/Q4 windows. Therefore this case must be treated as a **missed-opportunity / attention-continuity case**, not rewritten as a failed trade.

At the 2025-08-19 research checkpoint, TSM closed at **$232.70**. At that time, the market already knew from TSMC's April and July disclosures that AI-related demand was robust; TSMC expected AI-accelerator revenue to double in 2025 and approach a mid-40s percentage CAGR for the five-year period starting in 2024; CoWoS capacity was being doubled in 2025; 2025 capex was planned at $38-42B; Q2 revenue had reached $30.07B, +44.4% YoY in USD, with 58.6% gross margin and 49.6% operating margin. By late July, hyperscaler disclosures independently reinforced the multi-year demand signal: Alphabet raised 2025 capex to about $85B and expected a further increase in 2026, while Meta guided to $66-72B of 2025 capex and another year of similarly significant dollar growth in 2026 to add AI capacity.

The evidence therefore supports a precise diagnosis: **awareness existed; recognition existed; the main failure was persistent monitoring/event preparation and, secondarily, priority allocation.** The record does not establish that a fully formed buy decision was made and then ignored, so classifying the miss primarily as execution failure would overstate the evidence.

## Historical PIT timeline

### 2025-01-16 — pre-awareness evidence node / Discovery for the market

TSMC's 4Q24 release stated that continued AI-related demand would partly offset smartphone seasonality in 1Q25 and set 2025 capital budget at **$38-42B**. This was a company-reported demand/capacity signal, not yet a user-side documented decision.

**State:** Discovery / structural trend already visible.

### 2025-04-17 — major fundamental confirmation

TSMC reported Q1 revenue of **$25.53B**; HPC was **59%** of revenue; advanced nodes were **73%** of wafer revenue. Management reiterated 2025 revenue growth close to mid-20s in USD, said AI-related demand remained robust, reaffirmed that AI-accelerator revenue would **double in 2025**, said CoWoS capacity was being doubled, and gave an internal planning view that AI-accelerator revenue could approach a **mid-40s percentage CAGR for the five years starting in 2024**. TSMC also said its additional Arizona buildout was designed to support strong multi-year customer demand.

This is the strongest PIT evidence that the long AI-capex cycle was already publicly researchable by April 2025. It was management guidance/inference, not reported future fact.

**State:** Confirmation.

### 2025-07-17 — second confirmation / estimate-revision node

TSMC reported Q2 revenue **$30.07B**, +44.4% YoY in USD and +17.8% QoQ, gross margin **58.6%**, operating margin **49.6%**, and ADR EPS **$2.47**. Advanced technologies were **74%** of wafer revenue. Management guided Q3 revenue to **$31.8-33.0B** and explicitly attributed Q2 strength to continued robust AI/HPC demand.

**State:** Confirmation -> early Momentum.

### 2025-07-23 to 2025-07-30 — industry triangulation available before first recovered research date

Alphabet disclosed Q2 capex of **$22.4B**, raised 2025 capex outlook from $75B to about **$85B**, expected a further capex increase in 2026, and described cloud demand as remaining in a tight demand-supply environment into 2026. Meta guided to **$66-72B** of 2025 capex and expected another year of similarly significant capex-dollar growth in 2026 to bring AI capacity online. Microsoft's FY25 results showed **$64.55B** of additions to property and equipment and continued cloud/AI infrastructure investment.

These were independent primary-source signals supporting a multi-year compute infrastructure cycle. They did not guarantee TSM shareholder returns, but they materially reduced the probability that AI capex was only a one-quarter phenomenon.

### 2025-08-19 — first recovered user-side research record

TSM was explicitly included in the user's semiconductor/infrastructure tracking universe. IBKR historical data show an ADR close of **$232.70** that day.

At that point, the known information set included the April five-year AI-accelerator growth guidance, Q2 financial acceleration, large 2025 TSMC capex, CoWoS expansion, and hyperscaler 2025/2026 infrastructure plans.

A disciplined reconstruction would classify the security as **Confirmation / Momentum**, not Discovery. ## Core shareholder value chain

`AI/HPC end demand -> customer chip designs/tape-outs -> N3/N2/A16 + CoWoS demand -> installed leading-edge/packaging capacity -> utilization + mix + pricing -> revenue -> gross margin -> operating income -> operating cash flow -> capex -> free cash flow -> dividends / retained reinvestment -> shareholder value`

### Dominant variable at first recovered research date

The dominant variable was **whether the apparent AI infrastructure boom was durable enough to justify multi-year leading-edge and packaging capacity expansion without causing a later utilization/margin collapse**.

By April-August 2025, public evidence strongly supported durability: TSMC's five-year AI-accelerator CAGR framework, doubling of CoWoS capacity, high 2025 capex, robust Q2 growth, and hyperscaler 2026 capex language.

### Most easily misunderstood link

The most easily misunderstood link is **CapEx**. High capex is neither automatically bad nor automatically bullish. For TSMC it creates value only if the company preserves leading-edge demand, utilization, pricing/mix and returns on incremental capital. The correct object is `capex productivity`, not capex growth alone.

### What ultimately drove the stock from first recovered research date to current review

From $232.70 on 2025-08-19 to roughly $420 on 2026-08-18, the ADR price rose about **80.5%**. TTM ADR EPS rose from about **$8.77** to **$13.86**, or about **58%**, while trailing P/E expanded from about **26.5x** to **30.3x**, or about 14%. On a logarithmic decomposition, roughly **three-quarters to four-fifths** of the price move is attributable to earnings growth and the remainder to multiple expansion. This is an analytical attribution, not a causal market microstructure estimate.

Capital structure was not a material positive or negative driver because TSMC remained net cash with essentially flat share count. Market/sector beta and positioning likely contributed to path volatility, but this migration does not claim a precise residual attribution without a dedicated benchmark regression.

## Counterfactual TRQuant discipline

### Block an erroneous entry

Do not initiate merely because TSM is an AI beneficiary or the stock falls. Block if valuation has rerated without corresponding EPS/guidance revision, or if AI/HPC demand, customer capex, node execution, margin or geopolitical operating conditions deteriorate.

### Allow a small trial position

Permit a 10-15% research sleeve of the intended final TSM allocation when: thesis confidence is high, valuation is not extreme relative to observable earnings growth, and the next evidence nodes are defined.

### Add

Add when at least one of two paths occurs:

1. **Fundamental-upgrade path:** revenue/EPS guidance or hyperscaler/AI demand evidence rises enough to offset a higher price; or
2. **Price-reset path:** price/multiple compresses materially while the operating thesis and forward evidence remain intact.

### Trim

Trim when price/multiple expands materially faster than earnings estimates, portfolio AI concentration becomes excessive, or expected return compresses without new thesis evidence.

### Exit / full re-underwrite

Exit or materially reduce if leading-edge technology leadership is structurally impaired, major customers/designs migrate away, AI/HPC demand breaks for multiple quarters, capex remains high while utilization/revenue guidance falls and FCF deteriorates, or a geopolitical event impairs actual production/logistics/market access.

## Attention-continuity process rule

A core researched security must not disappear because human attention moves elsewhere.

For `P0 Persistent Monitor` cases:

`thesis established -> next evidence node scheduled -> event card prepared -> automated source check -> material-change gate -> decision queue -> explicit action/no-action record -> next evidence node`

Minimum TSM cadence:

- **Daily exception-only:** >7% single-day move, >12% drawdown from recent high, new 52-week high, export-control/customer/geopolitical event.
- **Monthly mandatory:** TSMC monthly revenue; compare three-month trend with quarterly guidance.
- **Quarterly mandatory:** revenue, HPC/platform mix, node mix, gross/operating margin, EPS, CFO, capex, FCF, inventory/AR, guidance, technology roadmap, customer concentration and valuation.
- **Industry triangulation:** material capex/guidance changes at the major cloud customers should create a TSM re-research event even if TSMC itself has not reported yet.
- **No silent decay:** if no review has occurred by the next scheduled evidence node, the case is marked `stale_attention` rather than disappearing from the queue.

## Candidate lessons

1. **Persistent attention is part of investment execution.** Once a security is thesis-ready, missed scheduled evidence nodes are a process failure unless explicitly deprioritized for a recorded reason.
2. **Widely followed does not mean fully priced.** TSM was already globally recognized in 2025, yet subsequent EPS growth materially exceeded the level embedded in the first recovered research-date price.
3. **For capital-intensive compounders, capex productivity is more informative than capex magnitude.**
4. **A missed winner is not automatically an execution error.** If no documented buy decision existed, diagnose preparation/monitoring/priority before execution.
5. **Industry capex disclosures can be upstream evidence nodes for bottleneck suppliers.** Hyperscaler 2026 capex guidance should have triggered TSM re-underwriting even without a TSMC-specific announcement.

These remain case-level candidates unless corroborated by additional Golden Cases.
