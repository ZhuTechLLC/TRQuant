## Founder-level decision summary

TSM is a proven, capital-intensive structural compounder. The shareholder-return question is no longer whether AI demand is real; it is whether AI/HPC demand, leading-edge process leadership, advanced packaging, utilization and pricing can continue to grow earnings faster than already-high expectations while absorbing very large capex, overseas-fab dilution and geopolitical tail risk.

The historical process lesson is attention continuity. The earliest recovered user-side explicit TSM research record is 2025-08-19, when the ADR closed at **$232.70**. By then TSMC had already disclosed robust AI/HPC demand, a plan for AI-accelerator revenue to double in 2025, a five-year planning view approaching a mid-40s percentage CAGR, doubling CoWoS capacity and $38-42B of 2025 capex. Alphabet and Meta had also signaled continued 2026 infrastructure investment. No verified contemporaneous TSM buy/add/trim/target/stop/size decision was recovered, so this is a missed-opportunity / attention-continuity case, not a failed trade rewritten with hindsight.

## Expectation-gap reconstruction

Recovered FY2026 sell-side ADR EPS consensus increased from approximately **$10.95** before the first recovered 2025 research date to approximately **$16.45** by the 2026-08-18 review, an increase of about **50%**.

From **$232.70** on 2025-08-19 to **$413.41** on 2026-08-18, TSM rose approximately **77.7%**. TTM ADR EPS rose about **58.0%**, while trailing P/E increased from about **26.53x** to about **29.83x**. On a log decomposition, approximately **80%** of the price move is associated with earnings growth and about **20%** with multiple expansion. This is an analytical bridge, not a causal market-microstructure model.

The central historical lesson is therefore precise: the market broadly recognized AI, but materially underestimated the **magnitude and duration of TSMC earnings conversion**. The user-side process failure was primarily persistent monitoring/event preparation, not failure to discover the theme.

## Core shareholder value chain

`AI/HPC end demand -> customer designs/tape-outs -> N3/N2/A16 + CoWoS workload -> installed capacity -> utilization/mix/pricing -> revenue -> gross margin -> operating income -> operating cash flow -> capex -> free cash flow -> reinvestment/dividends -> shareholder value`

The dominant variable is **capex productivity relative to expectations**. High capex is neither automatically bullish nor bearish. It creates value only if TSMC preserves demand, utilization, pricing/mix, margins and cash conversion.

## Action framework

### Start small / buy review

A small research sleeve can be considered only if:

- AI/HPC operating demand remains intact;
- FY2026/FY2027 EPS expectations and company guidance have not deteriorated materially;
- N2/A16 and advanced-packaging execution remain intact;
- the macro/rate/geopolitical overlay has not changed the downside asymmetry materially;
- portfolio AI/semiconductor concentration remains acceptable.

### Add

Add only through one of two paths:

1. **Fundamental upgrade:** revenue/EPS guidance or upstream demand evidence improves enough to raise expected value despite price; or
2. **Price reset:** valuation compresses while the operating thesis remains intact.

### Trim

Trim if the multiple expands materially faster than earnings estimates, portfolio AI exposure becomes excessive, or expected return compresses without new fundamental evidence.

### Thesis re-underwrite / exit

Re-underwrite materially if:

- multiple major customers reduce real AI compute spending for several quarters;
- N2/A16/advanced-packaging execution or yield leadership suffers structural impairment;
- major leading-edge designs migrate away;
- margins are persistently impaired beyond explained ramp/overseas-fab effects;
- capex remains high while utilization, guidance and FCF deteriorate;
- geopolitical events materially impair production, logistics, personnel, exports or market access.
