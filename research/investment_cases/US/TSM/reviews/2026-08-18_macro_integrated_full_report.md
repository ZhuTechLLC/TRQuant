## Executive conclusion

TSMC remains a high-quality structural AI-infrastructure bottleneck with a validated operating thesis, but the investment problem has shifted from theme discovery to expectation management. The strongest historical insight is that AI was already widely known by 2025-08-19; what the market underestimated was the magnitude and persistence of earnings conversion. Recovered FY2026 ADR EPS consensus increased from about $10.95 before the first explicit user research date to about $16.45 by 2026-08-18, while the stock rose from $232.70 to $413.41. Earnings revision, not multiple expansion alone, was the principal repricing engine.

The current macro state is bifurcated. AI/HPC operating demand remains GREEN, while the discount-rate and inflation overlay is YELLOW-RED. The July FOMC held the target rate at 3.50%-3.75%; June core PCE was 3.3% YoY; Q2 real GDP growth slowed to a 1.5% annualized rate; and the 10-year TIPS real yield was 2.41% on August 18. This mix raises the hurdle rate for a high-consensus growth asset without yet demonstrating a break in TSMC's underlying AI/HPC demand.

TSM closed August 18 at $413.41, about 13.7% below the 52-week high of $478.89. Using the current recovered FY2026 EPS consensus of $16.45, the close implies roughly 25.1x forward earnings. The price has therefore entered the existing TRQuant $395-$420 first-accumulation / buy-review zone. This is a re-underwrite trigger, not an automatic buy signal.

**Current action state: START SMALL / BUY REVIEW, conditional on thesis integrity and portfolio risk.**

## 1. Business and earnings engine

TSMC's dominant value driver is the durability and capital productivity of AI/HPC-driven leading-edge and advanced-packaging expansion. Q2 2026 revenue was $40.20B, gross margin 67.7%, operating margin 60.3%, and ADR EPS $4.31. Q3 revenue guidance was $44.6-$45.8B and management expected FY2026 USD revenue growth slightly above 40%. Advanced nodes remain the center of the mix, and N2 has begun contributing.

The relevant causal chain is:

AI/cloud monetization -> hyperscaler real compute capex -> GPU/custom-ASIC design demand -> leading-edge wafers + advanced packaging -> utilization/mix/pricing -> revenue/margins -> operating cash flow/capex/FCF -> EPS revisions -> valuation.

Capex is not bullish by itself. The correct test is whether utilization, margins, OCF and FCF validate incremental capital productivity.

## 2. Historical expectation gap

At the recovered first explicit research checkpoint on 2025-08-19, TSM closed at $232.70 and the latest recovered sell-side FY2026 ADR EPS consensus was about $10.95, implying roughly 21.25x forward earnings. By 2026-08-18 the recovered FY2026 consensus had risen to about $16.45, an increase of roughly 50%, while the stock price had risen materially.

This case therefore demonstrates a critical distinction: a structural theme can be widely recognized while the market still underestimates the magnitude and duration of future earnings. The missed edge was not 'discover AI'; it was to maintain a persistent evidence queue capable of observing repeated upward revisions and acting before the full magnitude was reflected in expectations.

## 3. Macro & cross-asset state

### Rates / discount rate — YELLOW-RED

The FOMC maintained the federal-funds target at 3.50%-3.75% on 2026-07-29. The 10-year Treasury par real yield was 2.41% on 2026-08-18. For TSM, this primarily acts through the valuation hurdle rather than directly through current foundry demand. A sustained move in the 10-year real yield above roughly 2.75%, especially if earnings revisions flatten, should force a valuation re-underwrite. A sustained move below roughly 2.0% can improve the valuation backdrop but should not override company evidence.

### Inflation — YELLOW-RED

June 2026 core PCE was 3.3% YoY and headline PCE 3.7% YoY. Sticky inflation raises the probability that restrictive real rates persist. The negative transmission is multiple compression and tighter risk budgets; the operating thesis is damaged only if inflation/policy ultimately weakens AI/cloud spending or materially changes customer economics.

### Growth — YELLOW

Q2 2026 US real GDP grew at a 1.5% annualized rate, down from 2.1% in Q1. Slower broad growth can pressure consumer/industrial semiconductor demand, but the key TSM question is whether AI/HPC remains decoupled. A broad slowdown alone is not a TSM kill signal; the red combination is slower growth plus weakening cloud/AI demand plus deterioration in TSM monthly revenue/guidance.

### AI infrastructure capex — GREEN

The recovered upstream evidence continues to show exceptional AI infrastructure spending and capacity requirements across major hyperscalers and accelerator suppliers. The correct monitoring unit is **real compute capex**, not headline nominal capex: GPUs/CPUs/accelerators/custom silicon should be separated from buildings, networking, leases and component-price inflation.

A RED re-underwrite requires at least two major hyperscalers to materially reduce real compute plans together with weakening cloud/AI demand and deterioration in TSM or accelerator evidence. One project timing change is insufficient.

### Semiconductor cycle / advanced-node utilization — GREEN

TSMC's own revenue, node mix, margins and guidance remain strong. The principal cyclical risk is not a generic semiconductor correction but a correction that reaches advanced AI/HPC nodes or packaging, causing utilization, pricing/mix and capital productivity to deteriorate.

### USD/TWD — YELLOW

FX affects earnings translation and ADR/local-share attribution. It must be monitored, especially for large moves, but should not be promoted into an automatic signal. The historical ADR/local attribution remains provisional until the canonical same-date FX series is completed.

### Export controls / industrial policy — YELLOW-RED

This is a direct operating channel. New rules can alter accelerator shipments, customer addressable markets, equipment access, fab location economics and overseas costs. The trigger is not political rhetoric; it is a rule or enforcement action with a material quantified effect on major customers/products or production capability.

### Taiwan geopolitical tail risk — STRUCTURAL RED TAIL, no current operating impairment established

The tail risk should remain explicit in the required return and position-size ceiling. A verified event impairing production, logistics, power, personnel, shipping or market access is an immediate kill/re-underwrite trigger. Exercises, rhetoric or news flow without operational impairment may affect the risk premium but are not equivalent to a destroyed thesis.

## 4. Current valuation and price state

2026-08-18 regular-session close: **$413.41**. 52-week high: **$478.89**. Drawdown: approximately **13.7%**. Current recovered FY2026 EPS consensus: **$16.45**, implying about **25.1x** forward earnings.

This is materially less stretched than the recent high but remains a high-consensus valuation. The correct decision variable is whether EPS expectations continue to rise faster than the valuation burden. A lower price with intact AI/HPC demand and intact margin/capital-productivity evidence improves expected return; a higher price without estimates/guidance moving with it reduces expected return.

Existing TRQuant price review zones are used only as action checkpoints:
- **$395-$420:** first accumulation / buy-review zone if thesis remains intact.
- **$370-$395:** stronger valuation-reset / add-review zone, but re-underwrite why the stock fell.
- **Below $370:** high-priority fundamental re-underwrite; a low price is not automatically an opportunity.
- **Around/above $480 without new EPS/guidance evidence:** do-not-chase / valuation-compression review.

These are not deterministic intrinsic-value targets.

## 5. Decision matrix

**Start Small / Buy Review:** current state. Thesis high confidence; price has reset into the first review zone; macro discount-rate pressure is elevated but operating evidence remains strong. Use a research-sized position, not full conviction, if portfolio semiconductor/AI concentration permits.

**Add:** either (a) further price/valuation compression with thesis intact, or (b) a fundamental upgrade in TSM guidance/EPS/upstream real compute demand that improves expected return even at a higher price.

**Hold:** evidence continues to validate AI/HPC growth, margins and capital productivity while valuation remains supportable.

**Reduce:** valuation expands faster than EPS estimates, correlated AI exposure becomes excessive, or expected return compresses without new evidence.

**Avoid / Exit / Re-underwrite:** two-or-more-hyperscaler real-compute cuts combined with demand deterioration; structural leading-edge/design loss; persistent unexplained margin/FCF impairment; capex remains high while utilization/guidance/FCF deteriorate; or verified geopolitical operating impairment.

## 6. Monitoring architecture

TSM is P0 persistent-monitor status. Monitoring must be exception-driven rather than notification-heavy.

Daily/continuous exceptions: >=7% one-day move with abnormal volume; >=12% drawdown from recent/52-week high; new high; material export-control/customer/geopolitical event; verified operations disruption.

Monthly mandatory: monthly revenue, three-month revenue trend, guide bridge.

Quarterly mandatory: revenue, HPC/platform mix, node mix, gross/operating margin, EPS, OCF, capex, FCF, inventory/AR, guidance, roadmap, customer concentration, valuation.

Upstream: NVIDIA/accelerator demand, GOOGL/META/MSFT/AMZN real-compute capex, custom ASIC mix, advanced packaging/HBM constraints, AI monetization/backlog and capacity constraints.

Macro: 10Y real yield, FOMC path, inflation/growth mix, USD/TWD, material energy/power constraints, export controls and Taiwan operational risk.

Known next nodes: 2026-08-26 NVIDIA earnings; 2026-09-10 TSMC August revenue; 2026-10-08 TSMC September revenue; each material hyperscaler earnings update; next FOMC/SEP.

TSM is a strong Golden-Case candidate for the TRQuant library because it combines structural growth, capital intensity, expectation revision and an identifiable attention-continuity failure. It should remain `research_complete`, not silently promoted to `decision_grade`, until the remaining valuation/FX provenance issues and founder/reviewer approval are resolved.

**Attention -> Evidence -> Earnings Revision -> Expectation Gap -> Valuation -> Action.**

Macro is an overlay on this chain and sometimes an operating driver, but it must remain causally explicit. A macro number by itself is never a trade signal.
