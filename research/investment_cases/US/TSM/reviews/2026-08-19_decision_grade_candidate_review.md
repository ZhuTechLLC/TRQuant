### Trim

Trim when:

- multiple expansion outruns EPS revisions;
- base-case 24-month annualized expected return falls below the portfolio hurdle;
- total AI/semiconductor factor concentration becomes excessive; or
- TSM-specific margin/capital-productivity evidence deteriorates even while sector demand remains strong.

## Kill criteria

A material thesis re-underwrite or exit is required if any of the following becomes verified and persistent:

1. multi-quarter AI/HPC demand deterioration together with meaningful cuts in major customers' real-compute plans;
2. structural N2/A16/advanced-packaging execution failure or major leading-edge customer/design loss;
3. persistent gross/operating-margin impairment without a credible recovery path;
4. capex remains high while utilization, revenue guidance and FCF deteriorate;
5. geopolitical events materially impair production, logistics, personnel, export capability or market access.
