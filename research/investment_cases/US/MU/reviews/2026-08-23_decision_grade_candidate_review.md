## Decision summary

**Current action: START SMALL — CONDITIONAL, not a full-size Buy.**

Micron's current operating evidence is exceptional: FY26 Q3 revenue, margin, EPS, operating cash flow and adjusted FCF accelerated dramatically; FY26 Q4 guidance implies further sequential growth; HBM4 is already in high-volume shipments; and multi-year take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements plausibly reduce the degree of historical memory-cycle mean reversion.

The stock nevertheless remains a cyclical/semi-cyclical underwrite. The current edge is not “AI memory demand is strong”; it is whether **normalized FY27-FY28 earnings remain far above old-cycle assumptions** after capacity responds.

## Reverse valuation

A 15% annualized two-year return from $966.78 requires a 2028 price of approximately **$1,278.57**.

Required 2028 EPS by exit P/E:

- 12x -> **$106.55**
- 10x -> **$127.86**
- 8x -> **$159.82**

This is the key current observation: the 8x hurdle is close to the current public FY2027-FY2028 consensus range. Therefore the security is attractive only if the new earnings regime is substantially durable; a reversion toward old-cycle earnings would create large downside despite the apparently low forward multiple.

## What would make the thesis more likely

1. FY26 Q4 actual results meet or exceed the ~$50bn revenue / ~$31 non-GAAP EPS guide without weaker pricing commentary.
2. FY27 DRAM/HBM and NAND demand-supply commentary supports sustained tightness rather than a rapid capacity-led normalization.
3. HBM4/HBM4E qualifications broaden beyond the lead platform and Micron retains technology/share momentum.
4. Strategic Customer Agreements continue to expand committed volume without evidence that contract pricing/terms transfer excessive economics back to customers.
5. Capex rises in a controlled manner while CFO/FCF and incremental ROIC remain strong.

## Counter-evidence / bear path

1. Mid-80% gross margins are extraordinary and can fall rapidly if supply catches demand.
2. The current stock price already reflects a historic earnings rerating and a large prior-year move.
3. Multi-year SCAs reduce uncertainty but do not eliminate technology, price, customer, cancellation/enforcement or industry-cycle risk.
4. HBM qualification/share can change faster than standard commodity-memory share.
5. Large new fab/capacity investment can eventually produce the very oversupply that compresses normalized earnings.

## Add / Hold / Trim logic

### Start Small
Permitted only if all remain true:
1. HBM4/HBM4E platform/qualification evidence remains intact.
2. FY27 expectation range remains roughly consistent with a normalized EPS path capable of meeting the reverse hurdle.
3. DRAM/HBM pricing and SCA evidence do not show a rapid deterioration.
4. Aggregate AI/semiconductor portfolio risk is acceptable.
5. The starter remains experimental, not a de facto full position.

### Add
Add only after one of two paths:
- **Fundamental confirmation:** Q4/FY27 evidence raises confidence that normalized EPS can remain around or above the current ~$150-160 expectation range while FCF remains strong; or
- **Price reset:** price falls enough that the 24-month base case materially exceeds the portfolio return hurdle without thesis deterioration.

### Hold
Hold when the structural-thesis evidence remains positive but normalized earnings and capacity-response uncertainty still prevent full sizing.

### Trim / reduce
Trim if:
- price/multiple expands materially faster than normalized FY27/FY28 EPS expectations;
- 24-month base-case annualized expected return falls below the portfolio hurdle;
- supply/capex evidence weakens normalized pricing before the market multiple adjusts;
- aggregate AI/memory/semiconductor factor concentration becomes excessive.

## Kill criteria

A material re-underwrite or exit is required if:
1. DRAM/HBM pricing and customer commitments weaken materially across multiple quarters;
2. margins mean-revert substantially faster than demand/supply evidence supports;
3. HBM4/HBM4E loses qualification/share at major AI platforms;
4. capex rises while FCF and incremental ROIC deteriorate, indicating emerging oversupply;
5. SCA economics/enforceability prove materially weaker than the disclosed take-or-pay structure implies.
