# NVDA FY27 Q2 Pre-Event Readiness — 2026-08-24

## Decision state
**WATCH / EVENT-READY. Default new capital before the report remains 0.**

The objective is not to predict the exact print. It is to define in advance which evidence would change the NVIDIA thesis and which evidence would propagate into TSM, MU, CRWV and the AI liquid-cooling cases.

## Known company guide
For FY27 Q2 NVIDIA guided:
- Revenue: **$91.0B +/-2%**.
- Non-GAAP gross margin: **75.0% +/-50bp**.
- The guide assumes **no China Data Center compute revenue**.

## Public market expectation entering the event
Third-party consensus snapshots on 2026-08-24 cluster around:
- Revenue: **~$91.8-$92.2B**.
- Adjusted EPS: **~$2.08-$2.09**.
- Data Center revenue: roughly **$85.7B** in one public consensus snapshot.
- FY27 Q3 revenue expectation: roughly **$104B**.
- Options imply approximately a **6%** post-earnings move.

These are market expectations, not issuer guidance.

## The trade turns more on the forward bridge than on a small Q2 beat
A Q2 print around $92B is close enough to company guidance that a small beat by itself should not be treated as a new thesis event. The more decision-relevant questions are:

1. **Q3 revenue guide versus roughly $104B market expectation.**
2. **Gross-margin durability around/above 75%** despite rising memory, system and networking content.
3. **Blackwell / Vera Rubin execution and supply** — whether shipment, networking and system ramps remain on schedule.
4. **AI demand quality** — whether hyperscaler, enterprise and sovereign demand supports continued real compute deployment rather than merely announced capex.
5. **China / export-control economics** — whether exclusion of China remains manageable or addressable demand changes materially.
6. **Capital / financing quality of AI customers and ecosystem** — whether infrastructure demand remains economically financeable rather than dependent on increasingly fragile circular financing.

## Pre-committed interpretation matrix

### Strong positive revision
More likely if several occur together:
- Q2 revenue clearly exceeds consensus rather than merely the company midpoint;
- Q3 guide is materially above the ~ $104B expectation;
- gross margin is >=75% with no adverse forward reset;
- Rubin/Blackwell and networking ramps remain on track;
- management describes broad demand with limited evidence of financing or deployment bottlenecks.

**Action implication:** immediate post-event re-underwrite for `START SMALL / BUY REVIEW`; do not auto-buy the gap.

### In-line / thesis intact
- Q2 near $91.8-$92.2B;
- Q3 guide around ~$103-$105B;
- gross margin near 75%;
- no material product, customer, China or financing deterioration.

**Action implication:** thesis remains strong, but stock attractiveness depends on price reaction and updated FY27/FY28 expectations. A strong price gap without a comparable estimate revision can worsen entry asymmetry.

### Mixed / caution
- Q2 beat but Q3 guide below the market's forward expectation;
- margin slips below the guided band or forward margin commentary weakens;
- evidence of customer deployment delays, networking/system constraints, or rising financing fragility.

**Action implication:** maintain WATCH; do not use a headline revenue beat as sufficient evidence.

### Negative thesis revision
- material guide miss or clear demand slowdown;
- persistent margin deterioration without a transition explanation;
- Blackwell/Rubin/networking execution impairment;
- multiple major customers reduce real compute plans;
- export controls reduce addressable demand beyond what current guidance assumptions already absorb.

**Action implication:** downgrade thesis confidence and propagate the change to TSM, MU, CRWV and AI liquid-cooling cases.

## Cross-case transmission

### TSM
Primary transmission:
`NVIDIA system/accelerator demand -> leading-edge + advanced-packaging workload -> TSM utilization/margins -> EPS revisions`.

A strong Q3 guide and intact Rubin ramp support TSM's decision-grade thesis; a weak real-compute guide would trigger a TSM re-underwrite even without a TSM-specific announcement.

### MU
Primary transmission:
`accelerator/system units + memory content -> HBM/DRAM demand -> pricing/mix -> normalized MU EPS`.

Pay special attention to any commentary on HBM supply, memory cost, system BOM pressure and customer deployment timing. NVIDIA strength does not automatically validate MU if memory supply discipline or competitive share deteriorates.

### CRWV
Primary transmission:
`AI demand + financing availability -> contracted capacity -> deployed capacity -> utilization -> shareholder cash conversion`.

Strong NVIDIA demand helps the top of the chain but does not resolve CRWV's financing, interest, capex and dilution bridge.

### AI liquid cooling / robotics thermal-management cohort
Primary transmission:
`next-generation rack power density + shipment scale -> liquid-cooling attach/content -> supplier batch revenue -> margin/CFO`.

Look for system-level power-density, rack architecture, liquid-cooling deployment and supply-chain commentary. Do not translate an NVIDIA platform statement directly into Feirongda/Lingyi/Tongxing orders without supplier evidence.
