# Alnylam Pharmaceuticals ALNY — Investment Case

**As of:** 2026-08-23  
**Status:** `research_complete`  
**Current action:** `START-SMALL / BUY REVIEW — CONDITIONAL`, no order created.

## Decision question
After the market punished Alnylam for lowering 2026 TTR guidance, does the current price understate the durability of AMVUTTRA's ATTR franchise, emerging operating profitability and pipeline optionality, or is the guidance cut an early sign that cardiomyopathy launch expectations were materially too high?

## What is established
- Q2 2026 total net product revenue was $1.172bn, +74% YoY; total TTR product revenue was $1.030bn, +89% YoY.
- Q2 total revenue was $1.291bn; GAAP operating income was $231m and GAAP net income $164m, versus an operating loss/net loss in Q2 2025.
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $3.3bn at June 30, up from $2.9bn at 2025 year-end, primarily from operating cash inflows.
- Management lowered FY2026 TTR net product revenue guidance from $4.4-4.7bn to $4.2-4.5bn and total net product revenue guidance from $4.9-5.3bn to $4.7-5.1bn, while collaboration/royalty guidance increased to $575-625m.
- Management attributed the TTR reset primarily to normalization of second-line volume after satisfying pent-up demand in the early ATTR-CM launch phase, while maintaining confidence in diagnosis/treatment growth.
- Direct IBKR 2026-08-21 close was $236.22; the direct 52-week high was ~$495.55 and low ~$197.81.

## Thesis
This is a **post-guidance-reset re-underwrite**, not a simple growth story. The bull case is that the market treated a normalization in launch sequencing as a structural franchise impairment even though product revenue is still growing rapidly and the company has crossed into GAAP operating profitability. The key is whether new ATTR-CM patient starts, diagnosis growth and persistence restore TTR growth without sacrificing economics.

## Counter-evidence
- The guidance cut is real and specifically targets the franchise that carries the valuation.
- Cost of goods sold rose to 25.4% of product revenue in Q2 from 21.1%, partly due to higher AMVUTTRA royalty rates; revenue growth does not automatically imply margin expansion.
- Commercial launch dynamics can remain volatile after pent-up demand normalizes.
- Pipeline programs add upside but also increase R&D spending and clinical/regulatory risk.
- Economic liabilities related to royalty/development funding mean cash alone should not be treated as simple net cash for valuation.

## Reverse valuation
Using the 2026-08-21 close and ~136m diluted share scale implies an approximate equity value of ~$32.1bn. Updated 2026 guidance implies total revenue components of roughly $5.275-5.725bn; midpoint ~$5.5bn.

For a 15% annualized two-year equity return, market value would need to reach about $42.5bn in 2028. That requires approximately:
- ~$7.08bn 2028 revenue at 6x sales (~13.4% CAGR from the 2026 midpoint);
- ~$8.49bn at 5x sales (~24.3% CAGR).

These are reverse hurdles, not forecasts. Because Q2 is already GAAP operating profitable, future valuation should progressively migrate from sales toward earnings/FCF as commercialization matures.

## Bull / Base / Bear
- **Bull:** ATTR-CM new-patient growth reaccelerates after pent-up demand normalization, AMVUTTRA establishes a durable full-spectrum franchise, operating leverage expands and pipeline readouts add value.
- **Base:** product growth remains strong but below original launch expectations; profitability improves gradually and valuation depends on evidence that the guidance reset is one-time rather than serial.
- **Bear:** TTR guidance is cut again, new-patient demand/persistence disappoints, competition or pricing reduces franchise value, or R&D/royalty economics prevent meaningful FCF scaling.

## Kill criteria
1. Another material TTR guidance cut without a temporary/mechanical explanation.
2. ATTR-CM new-patient starts, persistence or market share show structural weakness over multiple quarters.
3. Product gross economics deteriorate materially as AMVUTTRA scales.
4. GAAP operating profitability/cash generation reverses despite strong reported revenue growth.
5. A major safety/regulatory/competitive event impairs the TTR franchise or key pipeline value.

## Catalysts
- Ongoing ATTR-CM launch data and quarterly product-revenue progression.
- 2026 clinical updates including ALN-HTT02 and later RNAi Roundtables/program readouts.
- Evidence on zilebesiran/nucresiran and broader pipeline progression.
