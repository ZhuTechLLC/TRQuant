## 1. Research guardrail

This stage is eligibility-only. It intentionally calculates **no T+1/T+3/T+5 return, no MFE/MAE, no win/loss label, no strategy PnL and no order**. Event selection and mechanical filtering must be frozen before outcome extraction to reduce selection and look-ahead bias.

Mechanical eligibility rules used in v0.3:

1. Entry observation price at the first tradable minute at/after the planned entry time must be **>= $5**.
2. Median daily dollar volume over the **prior 20 completed daily bars** must be **>= $20 million**.
3. A ticker may contribute at most **2 candidate events** to the frozen manifest.
4. Data required to adjudicate the above tests must be available; otherwise status is `DATA_LIMITED` rather than an inferred pass.
5. Thresholds are not modified after seeing candidate identities or outcomes.

Category taxonomy:

- `E1` Clinical / Regulatory
- `E2` Earnings / Guidance
- `E3` Contract / Capacity

The first-stage target was to expand from 22 accepted events to roughly 40–50 mechanically eligible events without outcome peeking. v0.3 reaches **48 accepted events**, so the Stage-1 sample-size target is met.

This result is **not evidence that any catalyst strategy is profitable**. It only establishes a mechanically tradable, reasonably balanced sample on which the next PIT integrity checks can be performed.

## 7. Required next gate before outcome extraction

Before any return label is generated, freeze and audit for each of the 48 accepted events:

1. primary-source catalyst URL / document;
2. publication timestamp and timezone, when available;
3. session classification: premarket / regular-hours / after-hours / prior-day;
4. first admissible decision time under the protocol;
5. whether the planned entry timestamp occurs strictly after the information became public;
6. confounder flags: concurrent earnings, financing, M&A, macro shock, secondary offering, material follow-up disclosure;
7. source-quality grade and any unresolved timestamp ambiguity.

Only events passing this PIT provenance gate may enter M6.2-P2 outcome extraction. No event may be retained or rejected on the basis of its subsequent return.
