# M6.2-P2 Outcome / Path Extraction Protocol v0.1

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE OUTCOME EXTRACTION**  
Date: 2026-08-23  
Input sample: **46 M6.2-P1.5 `PIT_PASS` events only**  
Primary hypothesis: **H1 — Do complex, verified catalysts exhibit tradable 1–5 trading-day continuation after an accessible point-in-time entry, net of realistic costs?**

## 1. Anti-overfitting boundary

This protocol is frozen before P2 outcome extraction. P2 may not:

- add replacement events;
- alter the frozen decision timestamps;
- change the P1 price/liquidity gates;
- introduce GPT catalyst grades, semantic scores, analyst revisions, RVOL thresholds, support/resistance, RSI/MACD, PROBE/CONFIRM, or any other feature-selection layer;
- discard an event because its subsequent return is inconvenient;
- select the best-looking horizon after observing results.

P2 is a **simple H1 baseline**, not a production strategy optimization.

## 2. Frozen entry convention

For each event:

1. Use the frozen P1.5 decision timestamp in New York time.
2. Entry observation is the **close of the first valid regular-session one-minute trade bar whose `EndTime` is at or after the frozen decision timestamp**.
3. If the stock does not print immediately, retain the first later valid bar and record `entry_delay_minutes`; do not backfill a price from before the decision.
4. If no valid entry observation exists, mark the record `DATA_LIMITED`; do not substitute another timestamp.

This is an observable research entry proxy. P2 places no orders.

## 3. Frozen event-direction convention

No hand-labelled positive/negative catalyst score is used in H1.

Define the observable initial repricing at the decision point:

`shock = entry_price / prior_regular_session_close - 1`

Define the baseline direction solely from that observable price response:

- `direction = +1` if `shock > 0`
- `direction = -1` if `shock < 0`
- `direction = 0` only if the ratio is exactly unchanged

The H1 continuation quantity is therefore:

`directional_return_h = direction * (stock_close_h / entry_price - 1)`

This deliberately tests a low-complexity **post-catalyst price-continuation** hypothesis rather than a manually scored fundamental thesis.

## 4. Frozen horizons

`T+1`, `T+3`, and `T+5` mean the close of the 1st, 3rd, and 5th **completed regular U.S. trading sessions after the entry session**.

The primary H1 horizon is **T+3**. T+1 and T+5 are secondary horizon diagnostics and may not replace T+3 simply because they look better.

## 5. Benchmark

Primary ex-ante market benchmark: **SPY only**.

For each event, SPY holding-period return is measured from the first valid SPY minute at/after the stock's actual entry-bar `EndTime` to the SPY regular-session close on the corresponding T+1/T+3/T+5 date.

`directional_excess_h = direction * (stock_return_h - spy_return_h)`

Sector-adjusted returns are intentionally deferred; adding sector mapping before the simple H1 result would increase degrees of freedom.

## 6. Transaction-cost assumptions

No order is simulated in P2. Net research returns deduct fixed round-trip implementation costs:

- optimistic sensitivity: **10 bps**
- **primary assumption: 25 bps**
- stress sensitivity: **50 bps**

`net_directional_excess_h = directional_excess_h - round_trip_cost`

These are fixed before outcomes and are not tuned by category, ticker, direction, or horizon. Borrow availability/borrow fees for negative-direction observations are not assumed away; P2 reports direction counts and treats the short-side implementation issue as a limitation of this pilot.

## 7. Path metrics

For each horizon, calculate post-entry directional excursion using regular-session minute bars strictly after the entry bar, with the entry price as the initial path point:

`directional_path_return(t) = direction * (price(t) / entry_price - 1)`

- `MFE_h` = maximum directional path return from entry through horizon close.
- `MAE_h` = minimum directional path return from entry through horizon close.

The entry bar's pre-close high/low is not used for post-entry MFE/MAE.

## 8. Required event-level output

For every one of the 46 frozen IDs, persist at minimum:

- event ID, category, ticker, frozen decision time;
- actual entry bar time and `entry_delay_minutes`;
- entry price and prior-session close;
- `shock` and baseline `direction`;
- stock T+1/T+3/T+5 returns;
- SPY T+1/T+3/T+5 returns;
- directional raw and SPY-excess returns;
- net directional SPY-excess at 10/25/50 bps;
- MFE/MAE for T+1/T+3/T+5;
- missing-data / delayed-entry flags.

## 9. Primary H1 evaluation

Primary endpoint:

**Cross-event mean of T+3 net directional SPY-excess return using the frozen 25 bps round-trip cost.**

Required robustness checks, fixed in advance:

1. median T+3 net directional SPY-excess;
2. 10% trimmed mean T+3 net directional SPY-excess;
3. issuer-clustered bootstrap confidence interval for the T+3 mean;
4. T+1 and T+5 directional consistency;
5. contribution concentration — share of aggregate PnL-equivalent contribution from the top 3 events;
6. positive-return hit rate;
7. positive/negative direction counts;
8. results under 10 bps and 50 bps cost sensitivities.

No category-specific subset is promoted to the primary result in P2.

## 10. GO / INCONCLUSIVE / NO-GO gate

P2 does not call H1 validated from a positive sample mean alone.

- **GO:** primary T+3 25-bps net directional SPY-excess mean > 0; 10% trimmed mean > 0; issuer-clustered 95% bootstrap CI excludes 0 on the positive side; and T+1/T+5 diagnostics do not show a clear sign reversal that contradicts continuation.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** primary mean and trimmed mean are positive but the clustered 95% CI includes 0, or concentration/implementation issues prevent a defensible conclusion. Only one pre-specified sample-expansion step is then permitted; no feature mining.
- **NO-GO:** the primary mean is <= 0, or robustness diagnostics indicate that apparent continuation is dominated by a few events and does not survive the primary cost assumption. In that case H2/H3 are not opened.

## 11. Scope note

This 46-event study is a pilot causal/event-research sample, not a fully representative all-news universe. A `GO` result would justify broader out-of-sample replication; it would not by itself establish production-grade expected return.
