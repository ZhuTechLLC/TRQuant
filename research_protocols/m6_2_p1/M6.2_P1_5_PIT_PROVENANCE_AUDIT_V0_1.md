Research boundary: **NO post-entry return, MFE/MAE, win/loss label, strategy PnL, or outcome-dependent eligibility decision was inspected or used in this audit.**

## 1. Purpose

This gate tests whether each planned decision timestamp was actually point-in-time admissible. The question is not whether the event later produced a profitable move. The only question is whether a real-time investor could have known the catalyst before the frozen decision timestamp using public information.

The mechanical sample, decision timestamps, and liquidity thresholds are inherited unchanged from M6.2-P1 v0.3. An event is not rescued by moving its entry time after this audit.

## 2. PIT adjudication rules

### Status

- `PIT_PASS`: public availability before the frozen decision time is established with sufficient evidence.
- `PIT_AMBIGUOUS`: catalyst date/content is verified, but same-day public availability before the frozen decision time cannot be established with sufficient confidence.
- `PIT_FAIL`: the frozen decision time precedes the earliest independently verified public dissemination found in the targeted audit, or reliable evidence otherwise contradicts admissibility.

### Evidence modes

- `PASS_NEXT_DAY`: authoritative source is dated before the decision date; exact clock time is immaterial for the next-session decision.
- `PASS_INTRADAY_A`: issuer, regulator, or government source gives an explicit timestamp or a clearly pre-decision scheduled public event.
- `PASS_INTRADAY_B`: primary source verifies catalyst content/date and a credible contemporaneous distribution/news source verifies dissemination before the decision time.

### Source grades

- `A1`: issuer / investor-relations primary source with explicit time or clearly pre-decision webcast/call.
- `A2`: regulator / government primary source with explicit time.
- `B1`: primary catalyst source plus credible contemporaneous distribution timestamp.
- `B2`: authoritative primary source dated before a next-day decision; exact minute not required.

Confounder flags are recorded separately from PIT status. A confounder does not automatically make an event PIT-invalid; it determines whether the event belongs in the primary H1 sample or a later sensitivity analysis.

## 5. Non-primary records and anti-rescue rule

### `E3-GOOGL-20240426` — `PIT_AMBIGUOUS`

The catalyst itself is genuine and the Google primary page is dated Apr-26, but the primary page does not expose a publication minute and the audit did not establish that the $3B infrastructure announcement was public before the frozen **10:00 ET** decision. In addition, Alphabet had reported Q1 results after the prior close, creating an important concurrent-information confounder. It is retained in the audit trail but excluded from the primary P2 labels unless a pre-10:00 public timestamp is independently resolved without reference to subsequent price behavior.

### `E4-META-20240418` — `PIT_FAIL`

The primary Meta page is date-only. Contemporaneous independent reports found in the targeted search are timestamped **9:00 PDT / 12:00 EDT** and describe Llama 3 as being released then. Under the conservative admissibility rule, a frozen **10:00 ET** decision cannot be treated as post-publication. The event is excluded from P2. Its entry is **not** shifted to noon to rescue the record.

These exclusions are methodological, not performance-based; no subsequent return was consulted.
