# M6.2-P2E H1 Sample Expansion Protocol v0.1

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE NEW-EVENT OUTCOME EXTRACTION**  
Date: 2026-08-24  
Parent result: `M6.2_P2_H1_RESULTS_V0_1.md`  
Parent H1 status: **INCONCLUSIVE**  
Purpose: perform the single permitted out-of-pilot sample-expansion test before any H2/H3 feature research.

## 1. Research decision being tested

The pilot 46-event cohort produced a positive T+3 mean and positive 10% trimmed mean, but the issuer-clustered 95% CI crossed zero. Under the previously frozen anti-overfitting rule, exactly one sample-expansion step is permitted.

P2E therefore tests the same H1 on a **new expansion cohort**:

> Do complex, verified catalysts exhibit tradable 1–5 trading-day continuation after an accessible point-in-time entry, net of realistic costs?

P2E is a replication/precision step, not a feature-discovery step.

## 2. Final anti-rabbit-hole boundary

Before any new-event outcome is read, the following are locked:

- no GPT catalyst score;
- no semantic-complexity score;
- no RVOL threshold;
- no analyst-revision overlay;
- no sector filter;
- no support/resistance, RSI, MACD, moving-average or ORB rule;
- no PROBE/CONFIRM intraday state logic;
- no category-specific promotion based on the pilot's returns;
- no removal of expansion events because their realized return is inconvenient;
- no second sample-expansion round after P2E.

After a valid P2E run, H1 must resolve to `FINAL_GO` or `FINAL_NO_GO`. `INCONCLUSIVE` is not an allowed final research state unless the expansion itself fails the pre-specified execution-integrity minimum described below; in that case H2/H3 still remain closed.

## 3. Expansion size and balance

P2E adds exactly **125 candidate events** before mechanical/PIT exclusions:

- 25 E1 Clinical / Regulatory
- 25 E2 Earnings / Guidance
- 25 E3 Contract / Capacity
- 25 E4 Strategic / Product
- 25 E5 M&A / Policy

The 25 candidates in each category are allocated as exactly **5 candidates per calendar year** for each of 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.

This yields a fixed 5 categories × 5 years × 5 candidates = **125-candidate expansion grid**.

The original 50-candidate pilot manifest is not altered. The expected combined pre-filter manifest is therefore 175 candidate records, but **the expansion cohort is the primary P2E validation cohort**.

No category receives a larger quota because it performed well in the pilot.

## 4. Candidate-discovery principle

New candidates must be generated from source-defined event populations, not from remembered winners or from post-event price screens.

The discovery backbone is:

1. SEC 8-K / 6-K / material exhibit releases for U.S.-listed issuers where applicable;
2. FDA / regulator / U.S. government primary releases where the catalyst is regulatory, policy, award or public-private partnership driven;
3. issuer investor-relations releases only as a primary event source or as a timestamp/provenance verifier after a source-defined candidate has been identified.

General news articles may verify contemporaneous dissemination when the primary source lacks an exact clock time, but may not originate a candidate solely because the later price move is known.

## 5. Frozen category definitions

### E1 — Clinical / Regulatory

Material Phase 2/3 or pivotal clinical results, FDA approval/CRL/material regulatory decision, or equivalent high-impact medical regulatory event.

Discovery terms may include: `phase 3`, `pivotal`, `topline`, `primary endpoint`, `FDA approves`, `complete response letter`, `regulatory approval`.

### E2 — Earnings / Guidance

Earnings release, preliminary financial results, or a material guidance reset communicated by the issuer.

Primary SEC discovery includes 8-K Item 2.02 and attached earnings/guidance exhibits, plus 6-K equivalents.

### E3 — Contract / Capacity

Material customer contract, government award, long-duration purchase agreement, capacity commitment, major manufacturing/fab/data-center build commitment, or analogous economically material supply/demand commitment.

Discovery terms may include: `contract`, `award`, `purchase agreement`, `capacity`, `fab`, `data center`, `power purchase agreement`, `strategic supply`.

### E4 — Strategic / Product

Material product/platform launch, corporate separation/restructuring, strategic technology/product announcement, commercialization milestone, or similarly material strategic reset that is not primarily an earnings or M&A event.

Discovery terms may include: `launch`, `introduces`, `unveils`, `spin-off`, `separation`, `strategic review`, `restructuring`, `commercial launch`.

### E5 — M&A / Policy

Definitive acquisition/merger transaction or a material government/policy action with direct issuer-level economic impact.

Discovery terms may include: `definitive merger agreement`, `acquire`, `acquisition`, `merger`, `tariff`, `export restriction`, `government policy`, `grant`, `subsidy`.

Category assignment is based on the economic substance of the public catalyst, not on subsequent price behavior.

## 6. Deterministic candidate selection within each category-year cell

For each of the 25 category-year cells:

1. Build the source-defined candidate population for that cell using the frozen discovery sources and category definitions above.
2. Exclude records that are exact duplicates of the pilot manifest.
3. Canonicalize each candidate using:
   `category | ticker | first_public_date | primary_source_identifier`.
4. Compute a deterministic SHA-256 hash of the canonical string.
5. Sort ascending by hash.
6. Take the first five records that satisfy the issuer-frequency rule below.

Hash selection is used only to obtain an outcome-independent, reproducible sample spread. Hashes never use prices, returns, volume after the event, analyst reactions, or any outcome field.

If a source-defined category-year population contains fewer than five usable candidate records, retain all available records and mark the cell `SOURCE_POPULATION_SHORTFALL`. Do not borrow replacements from another year/category after outcomes are observed.

## 7. Frozen issuer-frequency rule

Across the **combined pilot + expansion candidate manifest**, one ticker may contribute at most **2 events**.

- If a ticker already has two pilot candidates, it is ineligible for P2E additions.
- If a ticker has one pilot candidate, at most one P2E candidate may be admitted.
- The hash-ranked list is traversed until the five permitted records for the category-year cell are filled or the source-defined population is exhausted.

This rule is identity-based and outcome-independent.

## 8. Frozen decision-time convention for new events

Decision timestamps are generated mechanically from verified public-availability time in New York time.

### Premarket / prior-day information

- If the catalyst is publicly available before 09:30 ET on a regular trading day, decision time = **10:00 ET that trading day**.
- If the catalyst became public after the prior session close and before the next session open, decision time = **10:00 ET next trading day**.
- If only the public calendar date is verifiable, no same-day decision is allowed; the earliest admissible decision is **10:00 ET on the next trading day**.

### Intraday information

- If verified public dissemination occurs from 09:30 ET through 15:00 ET, decision time = **30 minutes after the verified public timestamp**.
- If dissemination occurs after 15:00 ET, decision time = **10:00 ET next trading day**.

No event is rescued by shifting its decision timestamp after price outcomes are known.

## 9. Mechanical eligibility gate

The expansion reuses the pilot's mechanical gates unchanged:

1. first tradable minute observation at/after the planned decision time must be >= **$5**;
2. median daily dollar volume over the prior 20 completed daily bars must be >= **$20 million**;
3. required data must be available, otherwise `DATA_LIMITED`;
4. ticker frequency cap described above;
5. no post-entry outcome field may be calculated during this gate.

Mechanical failures remain permanently in the audit manifest and are not replaced after outcome inspection.

## 10. PIT provenance gate

The P1.5 status system is reused unchanged:

- `PIT_PASS`
- `PIT_AMBIGUOUS`
- `PIT_FAIL`

Evidence modes remain:

- `PASS_NEXT_DAY`
- `PASS_INTRADAY_A`
- `PASS_INTRADAY_B`

Source grades remain A1/A2/B1/B2 as defined in the pilot provenance audit.

Expansion records that fail or remain ambiguous are retained in the audit trail and excluded from primary outcome extraction. They are not replaced after their outcomes are known.

## 11. Confounder rule

Confounders are recorded before outcome extraction.

For E2, earnings/guidance is the core event and is not a confounder.

For E1/E3/E4/E5, a distinct, separately material issuer-specific catalyst released between the verified event time and the frozen decision time is flagged `MATERIAL_CONCURRENT_CATALYST`.

- If the two catalysts cannot be separated in public timing/economic substance, the record is excluded from the P2E primary cohort before outcomes and retained as an audit record.
- Broad market/macro news is recorded but does not by itself exclude the event because SPY remains the frozen benchmark.

## 12. Outcome protocol — unchanged from P2

For every P2E `PIT_PASS` primary event:

- entry = close of first valid regular-session one-minute bar with `EndTime >= decision_time`;
- initial `shock = entry_price / prior_regular_session_close - 1`;
- `direction = sign(shock)`;
- T+1/T+3/T+5 = closes of the 1st/3rd/5th completed U.S. regular sessions after entry session;
- benchmark = SPY from aligned entry observation to horizon close;
- primary cost = **25 bps round trip**;
- sensitivities = 10 bps and 50 bps;
- MFE/MAE use regular-session minute bars strictly after the entry bar;
- no orders are placed.

The primary endpoint remains:

**Mean T+3 net directional SPY-excess return at 25 bps cost.**

## 13. Primary validation cohort versus pooled estimate

This is a critical replication rule.

### Primary final H1 decision

Use **P2E expansion `PIT_PASS` events only**.

The 46 pilot events are not reused to determine `FINAL_GO` because their outcomes have already informed the decision to expand the sample.

### Secondary estimate

After the expansion-only decision is frozen, a pooled pilot + expansion estimate may be reported for precision/context only. It cannot overturn the expansion-only final H1 classification.

## 14. Required expansion-only diagnostics

Report, at minimum:

1. valid expansion `PIT_PASS` N;
2. category/year counts and issuer concentration;
3. positive/negative shock-direction counts;
4. T+1/T+3/T+5 net directional SPY-excess mean at 10/25/50 bps;
5. T+3 median and 10% trimmed mean at 25 bps;
6. issuer-clustered 95% bootstrap CI for T+3 mean;
7. T+1/T+3/T+5 hit rate;
8. top-3 and top-5 positive/absolute contribution shares;
9. MFE/MAE distribution diagnostics;
10. mechanical fail, PIT fail, PIT ambiguous and data-limited IDs.

No category-specific return table may be used to choose a winning sub-strategy before the final H1 decision. Category tables may be generated only as descriptive audit output after the primary result is frozen.

## 15. Execution-integrity minimum

The target is roughly 100+ new PIT-valid events from the 125 fixed candidates.

A valid P2E empirical adjudication requires at least **90 expansion `PIT_PASS` events**.

If fewer than 90 expansion events are PIT-valid/data-complete, classify the step as `DESIGN_NOT_EXECUTABLE_AT_REQUIRED_COVERAGE`; H2/H3 remain closed and no additional hand-picked expansion is permitted under M6.2.

## 16. Final H1 gate

P2E is the final H1 replication gate.

### `FINAL_GO`

All must hold on the **expansion-only** cohort:

- T+3 25-bps net directional SPY-excess mean > 0;
- T+3 10% trimmed mean > 0;
- issuer-clustered 95% bootstrap CI for T+3 mean excludes 0 on the positive side;
- T+1 and T+5 mean diagnostics do not show a clear sign reversal inconsistent with continuation;
- no single issuer or top three events make the result economically non-credible under the pre-specified concentration diagnostics.

If `FINAL_GO`, H2 may open only as an incremental-alpha test over this simple baseline.

### `FINAL_NO_GO`

If the expansion-only cohort satisfies the execution-integrity minimum but fails any required `FINAL_GO` condition, H1 is not validated and H2/H3 remain closed.

There is no second `INCONCLUSIVE` expansion round.

## 17. Interpretation boundary

Even `FINAL_GO` means only that a simple cross-sector post-catalyst continuation effect survived one materially larger out-of-pilot replication under the frozen protocol. It does not itself establish a production portfolio, optimal sizing, live slippage, borrow feasibility, capacity, or execution alpha.
