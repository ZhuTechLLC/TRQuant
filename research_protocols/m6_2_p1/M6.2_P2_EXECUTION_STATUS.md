## Why v0.1R exists

The initial P2 draft used event-window history extraction from `initialize`. That design could request data later than the algorithm's current simulated time and is therefore not accepted as an authoritative PIT implementation.

v0.1R preserves the frozen research protocol but changes the mechanics to an event-driven implementation:

- no future event history is requested from `initialize`;
- prior-session closes are learned from the live simulated minute stream;
- entries are captured from the first valid regular-session minute at/after the frozen decision timestamp;
- SPY is aligned to the actual entry observation;
- T+1/T+3/T+5 are counted using subsequent completed market sessions;
- MFE/MAE use only bars strictly after the entry bar;
- no orders are placed.

This is an implementation correction, not a change to sample construction, entry rules, horizons, costs, benchmark, or the H1 decision gate.
