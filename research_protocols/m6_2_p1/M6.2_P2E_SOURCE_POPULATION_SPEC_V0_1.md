# M6.2-P2E Source Population Specification v0.1

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE EXPANSION CANDIDATE HASH SELECTION**  
Date: 2026-08-24  
Parent protocol: `M6.2_P2E_SAMPLE_EXPANSION_PROTOCOL_V0_1.md`

## 1. Purpose

This document removes the remaining discretion in the phrase `source-defined candidate population` before any new-event outcome is inspected.

The P2E discovery universe is built from authoritative public disclosure infrastructure first, then event identity is hash-ranked. Price moves are never used to discover an event.

## 2. Primary discovery backbone — SEC EDGAR

For calendar years 2021–2025, build the filing population from SEC EDGAR archival indexes and/or the SEC bulk submissions archive.

Authoritative SEC documentation states that:

- EDGAR daily indexes are available under `/Archives/edgar/daily-index`;
- full indexes are available under `/Archives/edgar/full-index`;
- indexes identify company name, form type, CIK, filing date and filing path;
- the SEC bulk `submissions.zip` archive contains public filing history for all filers from the Submissions API;
- EDGAR filing detail pages expose filing `Accepted` timestamps and reported 8-K items.

P2E may use the SEC bulk submissions archive for efficient issuer/form enumeration and the EDGAR filing/archive document path for primary text/exhibit retrieval.

## 3. Filing forms admitted to the source population

Primary SEC forms:

- `8-K`
- `8-K/A` only when the amendment itself contains a new material event disclosure rather than a clerical correction
- `6-K` for foreign private issuers with U.S.-listed common equity

Forms are not admitted merely because they follow a large stock-price move.

## 4. Security/issuer scope

A filing can become a P2E source candidate only if the issuer maps to a U.S.-listed operating-company common equity that is addressable by the QuantConnect U.S. equity dataset at the frozen decision date.

Exclude from the discovery population before hashing:

- ETFs, ETNs and mutual funds;
- closed-end funds;
- SPAC shells before an operating-company combination;
- preferred-only listings;
- warrants/rights/units as the target security;
- OTC-only securities;
- issuers without a usable U.S. common-equity ticker mapping for the event date.

The later $5 and $20M mechanical trading gates are not applied during source discovery; they remain a separate frozen QuantConnect gate.

## 5. Filing timestamp

The SEC filing-detail `Accepted` timestamp is a valid authoritative public-availability timestamp for the SEC filing itself.

If an issuer/regulator primary release is independently verified to have become public earlier than the SEC acceptance time, the earlier primary timestamp may be used for the frozen decision-time rule, provided the provenance evidence is persisted.

If no earlier primary timestamp is established, SEC `Accepted` is the earliest admissible timestamp for that SEC-discovered disclosure.

## 6. Candidate text used for classification

For each admitted filing, construct a normalized text bundle from available material disclosure text in this order:

1. primary 8-K/6-K filing body;
2. press release / material exhibit such as `EX-99.1` or equivalent;
3. other event-specific exhibit only when required to understand the same disclosed catalyst.

Ignore boilerplate exhibits that do not define the catalyst.

Normalization is limited to deterministic text preparation: lower-casing, whitespace normalization and removal of HTML markup. No language-model score is used for candidate selection.

## 7. Frozen category admission rules

A filing enters at most one P2E category population. If multiple rules match, use the priority order below based on the disclosed economic substance:

`E5 M&A/Policy -> E2 Earnings/Guidance -> E1 Clinical/Regulatory -> E3 Contract/Capacity -> E4 Strategic/Product`

The priority order prevents one filing from entering multiple hash pools.

### E5 — M&A / Policy

Admit when the filing body/items/exhibits describe a definitive merger/acquisition agreement, completion of a material acquisition/disposition where the transaction announcement itself is the catalyst, or a material issuer-specific government policy action.

Strong SEC structure signals include Item `1.01`, Item `2.01`, Item `5.01`, plus transaction exhibits.

Required text concept for M&A branch: at least one of `merger agreement`, `definitive agreement`, `acquire`, `acquisition`, `merger`, `tender offer`.

Policy branch may additionally originate from the official government-source rule in section 8.

### E2 — Earnings / Guidance

Admit when:

- 8-K Item `2.02` is present; or
- a 6-K contains a financial-results release; or
- an issuer files a material preliminary-results/guidance reset outside a normal earnings release.

Required concept: `results`, `earnings`, `revenue`, `guidance`, `outlook`, or `preliminary results` in the event-specific filing/exhibit.

A routine periodic financial filing without a contemporaneous results/guidance catalyst is not a P2E E2 event.

### E1 — Clinical / Regulatory

Admit healthcare/biopharma disclosures describing material Phase 2/3 or pivotal results or a material FDA/regulatory decision.

Required concept includes at least one of:

- `phase 3`
- `phase iii`
- `pivotal`
- `topline`
- `primary endpoint`
- `fda approves`
- `approval`
- `complete response letter`
- `crl`

and the event text must identify a specific drug/device/program or regulatory action.

FDA/regulator-originated events may additionally enter through section 8.

### E3 — Contract / Capacity

Admit when the disclosure defines a materially specific economic commitment such as a major contract, award, purchase agreement, power purchase agreement, supply commitment, fab/manufacturing build, data-center build or other capacity commitment.

Required concept includes at least one of:

- `contract`
- `award`
- `purchase agreement`
- `power purchase agreement`
- `supply agreement`
- `capacity`
- `manufacturing facility`
- `fab`
- `data center`

and the event text must identify a named counterparty, project, committed amount/capacity, or other concrete transaction term. Generic statements about expected future capacity are insufficient.

### E4 — Strategic / Product

Admit material issuer-specific strategic/product events not already classified as E1/E2/E3/E5.

Required concept includes at least one of:

- `launch`
- `introduces`
- `unveils`
- `commercial launch`
- `spin-off`
- `spinoff`
- `separation`
- `restructuring`
- `strategic review`

The event must identify a specific product/platform/corporate action. Generic marketing releases are excluded.

## 8. Official non-SEC source supplements

To reduce category-specific SEC undercoverage, only the following authoritative source classes may supplement the SEC-discovered population:

### E1 supplement

- FDA official approval / complete-response / material regulatory release;
- other U.S. regulator primary release when directly governing the issuer's product.

### E3 supplement

- U.S. federal agency official contract/award/CHIPS/energy/infrastructure announcement naming the issuer or a controlled operating subsidiary.

### E5 supplement

- U.S. government/regulator official policy action naming the issuer or creating a directly attributable issuer-specific economic restriction/benefit.

Company IR may verify or provide earlier timing for these events but does not create a separate population duplicate when the same catalyst is already present through SEC/government sources.

## 9. Deduplication

Two source records are the same event when they refer to the same issuer and same economic catalyst within a 48-hour disclosure window.

Keep one canonical event record using source preference:

1. regulator/government primary source when the government action itself is the catalyst;
2. issuer/SEC disclosure when the issuer action is the catalyst;
3. earliest independently verified primary public timestamp for decision-time purposes.

All duplicate source identifiers remain in a provenance list but do not create extra hash entries.

## 10. Hash-pool construction

For each `category × calendar_year` cell:

1. enumerate all records admitted by sections 3–9;
2. remove exact pilot-event duplicates;
3. remove records that violate the frozen combined ticker cap before admission;
4. create canonical string:
   `category|ticker|YYYY-MM-DD|primary_source_identifier`;
5. compute SHA-256;
6. sort ascending by hash;
7. take the first five permitted records;
8. persist the complete ranked audit list or a bounded reproducible population artifact sufficient to reconstruct the five selections.

No price, return, post-event volume or analyst field is part of the hash pool.

## 11. Population completeness rule

A category-year cell is considered discovery-complete only when the defined SEC archive period has been fully enumerated for that year and all allowed official supplemental feeds for that category/year have been checked.

Do not stop searching after five attractive candidates are found. The hash selection is applied only after the cell's source-defined population is complete enough to satisfy the frozen enumeration procedure.

## 12. Outcome firewall

Until the 125-candidate expansion manifest is frozen:

- do not query T+1/T+3/T+5 returns for any expansion candidate;
- do not inspect event-window charts for selection purposes;
- do not use realized gaps, RVOL or option moves to decide candidate inclusion;
- do not replace a candidate after learning its return.

After manifest freeze, the existing mechanical/PIT gates may run, still without outcome extraction. Only after those gates are frozen may P2E outcomes be calculated.
